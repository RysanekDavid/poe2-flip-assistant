## 1. Runes of Aldur — the league mechanic

**Core loop** [CONFIRMED — 3+ sources: [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/runes-of-aldur), [fextralife](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Runes+of+Aldur), [gamerant](https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-2-poe2-runes-of-aldur-league-mechanic-explained/)]:
- Every area (Act 1 onward, campaign and endgame) can spawn an **Ezomyte Remnant** — a stone slab with empty runic slots.
- Socket 2–10 Runes from your rune tome into the slots to finish a "runic recipe." Longer recipes = more slots filled = tougher wave = better reward.
- Completing the recipe spawns **Runic monsters** empowered by the specific runes used — their granted abilities/properties depend on which runes were chosen — around the Remnant. Kill them to claim the reward tied to the recipe.
- Thematically the mechanic is framed as Kalguuran runesmithing techniques applied via Farrow to old Ezomyte Remnants — sources use "Ezomyte Remnant" as the object name and "Kalguuran" as the runesmithing/skill-tree branding; treat these as the same system, not competing mechanics [single-source — [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/runes-of-aldur) describes "ancient Ezomyte Remnants" and "Runic Magic of Kalguuran origin" as one mechanic; accessed 2026-10-01].

**Verisium** [CONFIRMED — [gamerant](https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-2-poe2-runes-of-aldur-league-mechanic-explained/), [fextralife](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Runes+of+Aldur)]: a metal that drops from **all** Remnants regardless of which reward you picked. Spent at the **Verisium Anvil** (towns/hideout) to:
  - Convert armour pieces (helmet/gloves/boots/chest) into **Runeforged** variants — they trade some flat defensive stats for **Runic Ward**, a supplementary damage-absorption pool that depletes before Life.
  - Upgrade **any Unique weapon** in the game — Runeforging raises its effective base/level requirement rather than granting Ward, making leveling uniques viable much longer [single-source — gamerant].
  - Add Ward to gear generally, per maxroll's independent description [single-source — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/runes-of-aldur-overview)].

**Kalguuran skill gems** [CONFIRMED — gamerant, [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/runes-of-aldur): "These Skills also have no Colour, no Attribute requirements" and "require Ward to be used"]: new skill gems with **no attribute requirements** that consume **Runic Ward** instead of mana to activate.

**Alloys** [CONFIRMED — gamerant, fextralife (13 types)]: currency items that remove a random affix on gear and replace it with a **guaranteed** one — functionally an Essence-adjacent, targeted-affix crafting tool, applied per equipment slot family.

**Legacy Runes / Aldur's Legacy** [single-source — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/runes-of-aldur-overview)]: a system that lets you destroy a Unique item to convert it into a powerful "Legacy Rune." Not corroborated elsewhere — treat as real but under-documented.

**Fluxes** [verified-primary — [poe2db Blazing_Flux](https://poe2db.tw/us/Blazing_Flux) accessed 2026-10-01: "Transforms all Cold and Lightning Resistance modifiers on an item to equivalent Fire Resistance modifiers … left click on a magic or rare item to apply it", stack size 10, drop level 65]: Runes of Aldur currency that **converts** existing resistance modifiers on an item to a different type. Fluxes do **not add** a resistance that isn't already present — the text only transforms modifiers already on the item. Obtained from Runeshape combinations at a Remnant (poe2db lists Blazing Flux from Fire + Earth + Prismatic + Soul + Rage + Rebirth runes, Lv70+) or bought on trade; the recipes for the other Fluxes were not checked.

**Saga items** — Omens (stack size 10, drop level 78) that alter the next **Expedition Logbook** you open, turning ordinary Grand Expedition biomes into scripted encounters [verified-primary — poe2db item pages [Aldur's_Saga](https://poe2db.tw/us/Aldurs_Saga), [Medveds_Saga](https://poe2db.tw/us/Medveds_Saga), [Uhtreds_Saga](https://poe2db.tw/us/Uhtreds_Saga), [Voranas_Saga](https://poe2db.tw/us/Voranas_Saga), [Olroths_Saga](https://poe2db.tw/us/Olroths_Saga), accessed 2026-10-01: class Omen, stack size 10, drop level 78, and each recipe below as a Lv70+ Runeshape combination]:

|---|---|---|

## 2. Essences

**Tier system** [CONFIRMED — [poe2db Essence](https://poe2db.tw/us/Essence) accessed 2026-10-01, [maxroll corruption outcomes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/corruption-outcomes), [poe2wiki](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Essence_of_Flames)]: 0.5 uses **Lesser / regular / Greater / Perfect** tiers plus four **Corrupted** essences (Hysteria, Delirium, Horror, Insanity). The corrupted ones come from Vaal-orbing an imprisoned essence monster: "Greater Essences are upgraded to Corrupted Essences; Hysteria, Delirium, Horror and Insanity" (maxroll).
- Items can hold **only one** Essence-granted modifier at a time in 0.5 [single-source — mobalytics search summary].
- 19 base Essence types, each guarantees a specific modifier family.
- **Imprisoned Essence monsters**: "Essence monsters are powerful monsters trapped in crystallised corruption. Breaking these monsters free allows them to be defeated, dropping the Essence" [verified-primary — [poe2db Essence](https://poe2db.tw/us/Essence)]. They appear from the Act 1 campaign through the endgame Atlas [single-source — poe2wiki].
- **Using a Vaal Orb on an imprisoned monster** "unpredictably modif[ies] the Essences it possesses": quantity changes, rerolls, upgrades to Greater, and Greater upgrading to a **Corrupted Essence** — the chase tier [single-source — [maxroll corruption outcomes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/corruption-outcomes)]. The often-quoted ~1% chance for the Corrupted outcome is [unverified].
- The 4 corrupted essences: **Hysteria** (a different mod per slot), **Horror** (increased effect of socketed augments, gloves/boots), **Delirium** ("Allocates a random Notable Passive Skill", body armour), **Insanity** (enchantments on corruption, belts) [verified-primary — [poe2db Essence](https://poe2db.tw/us/Essence)]. Earlier Exalted prices for them came from a gold-seller page and were removed — check poe.ninja.
- Essences are also purchasable from the **Currency Exchange NPC** per-Act, unlocked in Cruel difficulty after finishing Act 3 Normal [single-source — search-summarized game8/poe2wiki].
- Specific essence-type → specific monster/zone mapping was **not found** in sources searched — essences appear to be generic imprisoned-monster drops rather than zone-locked, contradicting a monster-specific loot-table assumption. Flag as open question.

## 3. Strongboxes

[CONFIRMED spawn-rate change — [switchbladegaming](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/path-of-exile-2/strongbox-guide/) + independently corroborated framing in search-aggregated results]: In 0.5.0, GGG **lowered base spawn rates** for Strongboxes (and Essences, Shrines, other map content) because the redesigned Atlas Passive Tree now provides much larger bonus availability — i.e., strongbox density is now Atlas-tree-gated, not baseline.

**Tier ranking by currency efficiency** [single-source — switchbladegaming]:
- **S**: Researcher's — always prioritize for currency; roll "of Bounty" (IIQ).
- **A**: Cartographer's (Atlas/Waystone sustain), Ornate (SSF/base crafting, "of Treasures" IIR).
- **B**: Jeweller's (accessory hunting), Large (generic fallback).
- **C**: Blacksmith's, Armourer's, Arcane (base-type-specific only).
- **D**: Standard — skip entirely.

**Numbers** [single-source, small sample — switchbladegaming]: community 60-map test with a minimal Atlas setup averaged **~0.5 Exalted Orbs per map** prioritizing Researcher's boxes.

**Farming recipe** [single-source — switchbladegaming]: take the "Likely Ambush" Atlas node (mandatory given lowered 0.5.0 base rates) + "Researcher's Plunder" (+150% conversion to Researcher's variant); roll boxes to Rare with Alchemy Orbs; add suffixes with Exalted only if prefixes allow; prioritize IIQ over IIR on currency boxes. Sweet spot for character IIR is **100–130%** (diminishing returns above); Atlas IIR keeps scaling without the same penalty curve.

## 4. Shrines

[CONFIRMED — [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/shrines), poe2wiki]: Shrines (aka Precursor Artifacts) buff nearby monsters; killing them and interacting grants the buff to the player, typically lasting **45s** by default.
- **Places of Worship** (Atlas passive): +100% chance for maps to contain Shrines.
- **Prayer for Guidance**: +100% chance Shrines are **Seek Shrines** (item rarity).
- **Faith in Luck**: 25% chance for a Shrine to roll an additional effect (raises odds of getting **Speed Shrines**).
- Meta strategy: stack Places of Worship to pair Speed/Seek Shrines with Strongbox farming for faster clears + higher rarity output [single-source — original combined search summary, switchbladegaming-adjacent].

## 5. Azmerian Wisps (core mechanic, active during Runes of Aldur)

[CONFIRMED — [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/azmerian-wisps), [vulkk 0.5.2 notes](https://vulkk.com/2026/06/12/path-of-exile-2-update-0-5-2-changes-overview/)]: Not a new 0.5 mechanic — Wisps originate from an earlier patch's league ("Dawn of the Hunt") and are now a **permanent core Atlas mechanic** that keeps running during Runes of Aldur.
- Wisps flee from the player, speeding up when approached and slowing when you back off; despawn if you lose them too long.
- A Wisp empowers monsters it passes through with animal-type buffs, then possesses a Rare/Unique monster, granting it a new ability.
- Killing a Wisp-possessed monster is the **only source of Talismans** — consumables socketed into gear Rune sockets.
- **0.5.2 "On the Wind" Atlas Keystone** [single-source — vulkk]: +50% Wisp movement speed (good for fast clear builds) or removes the despawn timer entirely (good for slow builds) — pick one.

## 6. Trials (Sekhemas + Chaos) — trade-relevant loot

### Trial of the Sekhemas
[single-source, one deep fetch — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/trial-of-the-sekhemas), corroborated at title/topic level by conquestcapped in search snippets]
- **Access**: Djinn Barya (drops from monsters starting Act 2). Floors scale with area level: lvl 24–44 → 1 floor, 45–59 → 2, 60–74 → 3, 75+ → 4 floors.
- **Honour**: a secondary "health" bar; hitting 0 ends the run. Armour reduces Honour loss, Evasion prevents it outright; Shrines inside the Trial restore it; "Honour Resistance" stat mitigates loss.
- **Sacred Water**: run-scoped currency spent at Merchants on Boons and Relics; unspent Sacred Water converts to extra Keys at the Keth Forge.
- **Relics**: meta-progression items slotted into a Relic Altar pre-run; craftable to Magic rarity; 3 relic sizes with distinct affix pools.
- **Zarokh, The Temporal** (final boss): always drops one Unique Relic; killing him under the right Unique Relic condition unlocks a themed reward.
- **Unique Relic → reward pairs**: The Burden of Leadership → Sekhema's Resolve, which since 0.5.0 means one of three rings — "The Sekhema's Resolve Unique Rings have been renamed to Safrin's Resolve, Zaida's Longevity, and Eshtera's Path" (corrected 2026-09-29) [verified-primary — [poe2db Eshteras_Path](https://poe2db.tw/us/Eshteras_Path) version history, [poe2db Zarokh](https://poe2db.tw/us/Zarokh%2C_the_Temporal), accessed 2026-09-29; the relic text still uses the old name]; The Changing Seasons → Sandstorm Visage; The Desperate Alliance → Against the Darkness (jewel); The Peacemaker's Draught → extra Djinn Barya; The Remembered Tales → Blessed Bonds; **The Last Flame → Temporalis** (sets max Honour to 1 as the drawback — high-skill, high-reward run).
- **Trade-relevant drops**: Time-Lost Jewels (exclusive to this content), Grand Spectrum Jewels, standard jewels from reward chests. No "soul cores" drop from Sekhemas — those are Trial of Chaos exclusive (see below); the domain brief's assumption that Sekhemas drops soul cores is **not supported** by sources.

### Trial of Chaos
[single-source deep fetch — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/trial-of-chaos)]
- **Access**: Inscribed Ultimatum item, drops from monsters starting Act 3. Rounds scale with area level: 39–59 → 4 rounds, 60–74 → 7 rounds, 75+ → 10 rounds.
- **Format**: pick one persistent negative modifier before each round; fixed sequence of kill/survive/escort/boss encounters; **failing any challenge loses all loot gathered that run** (all-or-nothing, unlike most PoE endgame content).
- **Soul Cores** are the headline reward, dropped at rounds 4, 7, and 10 (boss rounds). 15 varieties exist. **Soul Core of Azcapa** and **Soul Core of Zalatl** are called out as so valuable that community advice is to abandon (bank) the run immediately on getting one rather than risk losing it to a later failure [single-source — maxroll].
- **Bahlak, The Sky Seer** appears at round 10 and drops one of the 3 "Fates" needed to unlock the pinnacle fight.
- **Trialmaster access** requires all 3 Fates: Cowardly Fate (Uxmal, The Beastlord), Deadly Fate (Bahlak, The Sky Seer), Victorious Fate (Chetza, The Feathered Plague).
- **Trialmaster unique drops**: Glimpse of Chaos, Zerphi's Genesis, Mahuxotl's Machination, Hateforge, The Adorned, or corrupted Inscribed Ultimatums (i.e., the access item itself can drop corrupted, letting you re-roll a harder/better run).

## 7. Corruption Altars / Vaal side areas

[sources per bullet; corrected 2026-10-01]:
- **Vaal Ruins (Act 2)**: reported as the earliest Altar of Corruption, with free experimental corruption on campaign items [unverified — the only source was a gold-seller page; poe2db and maxroll name only the Act 3 altar].

- **Atziri's Temple** (not PoE1's Temple of Atzoatl; area id `IncursionTemple`): Corruption Chamber rooms Crimson Hall (Tier 1) and Catalyst of Corruption (Tier 2) hold a Corruption Altar ("Modifies an item unpredictably and Corrupts it", costs a Vaal Orb). The Tier 3 **Locus of Corruption** room holds the **Corruption Instiller**, which "Modifies a Corrupted Equipment or Jewel item unpredictably or destroys it" and takes an **Architect's Orb**; the room destabilises once used [verified-primary — [poe2db Atziris_Temple](https://poe2db.tw/us/Atziris_Temple), accessed 2026-10-01]. It is a second corruption pass on an already-corrupted item that can destroy it, not a guaranteed "two outcomes at once".

The claim that PoE2's Vaal Orbs skew toward stronger outcomes than PoE1's is [unverified]. Standard practice: finish all other crafting (quality, sockets, mods) before corrupting, since corruption is the last, irreversible step.

## 9. Gambling vendor (Alva)

**Not a 0.5 addition.** Gambling mechanic [single-source — [mobalytics gambling](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/gambling), accessed 2026-10-01]: an NPC takes gold and gives an item of the class you choose, usually magic, sometimes rare; uniques are possible "but are too rare for this to be useful". You can gamble "once per unique monster that you kill in the Act", and an Act's gambles can't exceed the item level of that Act's hardest zone. That the Act 3 vendor is Alva at the Ziggurat Encampment with items up to ~ilvl 80, that stored purchases cap at 100, and that only **ground-drop pool** uniques (never boss-exclusive ones) can come from gambling is [unverified] — the only source was a gold-seller page. Note: PoE1's **Gwennen** (level-35+ gambling vendor, rarity revealed post-purchase) is a different game's NPC and does not exist in PoE2 — don't conflate the two when reading older guides.

## 10. Uncut gems economy (level 20 gems, spirit gems)

[single-source — [mobalytics high-level gems](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/high-level-gems), accessed 2026-10-01; its odds are an Early Access datamine]:
- Level 20 gems only drop from **item level 82+** loot tables (zone level 81+ effectively), and even then there's a strong bias toward level 18 — level 20 is a tail outcome. Datamined share of uncut gems that are level 20: **1 in 81 (~1.2%)** at ilvl 82, 1 in 33 at ilvl 83, **1 in 19 (~5.3%)** at ilvl 84 [single-source, Early Access data — treat as order-of-magnitude for 0.5].
- Farm: **T15/T16 maps** that are **irradiated and on corrupted ground**.
- **Spirit gems** function like skill gems but are much rarer and reportedly trade at **~3x** the price of the equivalent-level normal skill gem [single-source — mobalytics].
- Early-0.5 currency tip repeated across guides: new/returning players should **bank low-level uncut spirit/skill gems** rather than using or vendoring them — some command real currency due to spirit-gem scarcity.
- Could not pull live price data from poe2scout.com/economy/uncutgems (page requires JS rendering not captured by fetch) — no hard current chaos/exalt/divine numbers obtained; treat gem pricing as directionally true but numerically unverified for this doc.

## Wallet warnings

- **Fluxes only retype existing resistances — they never add a missing one.** Buying a Flux to "fix" an item's resistance without first confirming that resistance type is already rolled on it is a wasted purchase.
- **Trial of Chaos is all-or-nothing**: failing any single challenge in a 10-round run **loses everything gathered**, including a banked Soul Core of Azcapa/Zalatl if you didn't bank it early — community advice is to voluntarily end the run the moment you get a top-tier Soul Core rather than push further rounds.
- **Vaal Orb corruption is irreversible** — finish all other crafting (quality, sockets/socket colors, affixes) before corrupting; corrupted items cannot be further modified by normal currency.
- **Don't gamble-farm for chase uniques** — uniques from gambling are "too rare for this to be useful" (mobalytics); the further claim that boss-exclusive uniques can never come from gambling is [unverified].
- **Strongbox base spawn rates were nerfed in 0.5.0** — running old (pre-0.5) Atlas setups without the "Likely Ambush" / strongbox-boosting nodes will undershoot expected currency income; the Atlas tree, not baseline map spawns, is now the primary lever.
- **Buying Aldur's Sagas off trade to farm Grand Expeditions may not math out** — the currency cost of bought Sagas can exceed the expected value of the boosted Logbook outcome unless run in volume with an optimized setup [single-source — the mobalytics profile guide cited under Profit angles, which warns the math breaks at trade prices; the "10-15+ at once" batch size came from a gold-seller page and was dropped].
- **Level 20 gem farming has a real floor** — you need ilvl 82+ content (irradiated/corrupted T15-16 maps), not just "high tier maps." Farming lower-tier content for level 20 gems is close to a zero-EV loop.
- **The pinnacle Arbiter of Divinity needs both the Matriarch and Patriarch fights first** (Origin Spark + Origin Cradle → Origin Core; maxroll, §8) — don't buy into it without clearing those reliably. A further Arbiter of Ash prerequisite and T15 Waystone gate are [unverified].

## Profit angles

- **Researcher's Strongbox farming**: Atlas-invest into "Likely Ambush" + "Researcher's Plunder," roll boxes to Rare with IIQ suffixes via Exalted/Alchemy — reported ~0.5 Exalted/map baseline in a 60-map sample; scale by running more maps/hour rather than chasing bigger single hits [single-source numbers — switchbladegaming].
- **Uhtred's Saga rush-farming**: craft/acquire Uhtred's Saga (cheap) → guarantee an Uhtred, the Stardrinker encounter on your next Logbook → chase the **Depleted Mana Rune**, which becomes **Runeseeker's Call** after charging it with 10,000 mana and handing it to Dannig (Act 4) [verified-primary chain — poe2db, §1]. Runeseeker's Call's price (quoted at ~700–900 Divine) and the rune's drop chance are [unverified]; check trade before investing. Highly RNG-dependent.
- **Aldur's Saga volume farming**: run 10-15+ Sagas in a batch against Grand Expedition Logbooks for Verisium Remnant loot + high-slot Alloy rolls; one source estimates **60-100 Divine/hour** for an optimized volume setup, but explicitly warns the math breaks if you're buying Sagas at trade price rather than self-farming them [single-source, wide claimed range — mobalytics profile guide].
- **Corrupted Essence farming**: Vaal Orb imprisoned monsters holding Greater Essences for a chance that they upgrade to a Corrupted Essence [single-source — maxroll, §2]. The hit rate (~1% was quoted) and which corrupted essence is worth chasing are [unverified] — check poe.ninja before treating it as a profit loop.
- **Trial of Chaos Soul Core runs**: farm Inscribed Ultimatums, push to round 10 for 3 Soul Core shots per run; Azcapa/Zalatl are valuable enough that community practice is "extract on hit" rather than push for the Trialmaster fight itself unless already banked a lesser core.
- **Locus of Corruption (Atziri's Temple, Tier 3 Corruption Chamber)** second-corruption play: the Corruption Instiller modifies an already-corrupted equipment piece or jewel unpredictably **or destroys it**, costing an Architect's Orb (poe2db, §7). Any resale premium for such items in 0.5 is inference, not sourced — only risk items you can afford to lose.
- **Level 20 / Spirit gem flipping**: bank uncut spirit and skill gems early rather than vendor them; spirit gems trade ~3x their skill-gem-level equivalent — cheap to store, meaningful upside if the specific gem is chase-tier for a popular build.
