# PoE2 Remaining 0.5 Mechanics — Runes of Aldur League, Essences, Strongboxes, Shrines, Trials, Pinnacle Bosses, Corruption, Gems
**Patch stamp:** Path of Exile 2, patch 0.5.x "Return of the Ancients," league **Runes of Aldur**, live since 2026-05-29, current through hotfixes up to 0.5.4 (as of 2026-07-14). Facts below are dated to the specific 0.5.x point release where sources specify it; assume 0.5.0 baseline otherwise.

## 1. Runes of Aldur — the league mechanic

**Core loop** [CONFIRMED — 3+ sources: [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/runes-of-aldur), [fextralife](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Runes+of+Aldur), [gamerant](https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-2-poe2-runes-of-aldur-league-mechanic-explained/), [rpgstash](https://www.rpgstash.com/blog/poe-2-runes-of-aldur-guide)]:
- Every area (Act 1 onward, campaign and endgame) can spawn an **Ezomyte Remnant** — a stone slab with empty runic slots.
- Socket 2–10 Runes from your rune tome into the slots to finish a "runic recipe." Longer recipes = more slots filled = tougher wave = better reward.
- Completing the recipe spawns **Runic monsters** empowered by the specific runes used — their granted abilities/properties depend on which runes were chosen — around the Remnant. Kill them to claim the reward tied to the recipe.
- Thematically the mechanic is framed as Kalguuran runesmithing techniques applied via Farrow to old Ezomyte Remnants — sources use "Ezomyte Remnant" as the object name and "Kalguuran" as the runesmithing/skill-tree branding; treat these as the same system, not competing mechanics [single-source reconciliation, no source directly states they're identical but no source contradicts — [rpgstash](https://www.rpgstash.com/blog/poe-2-runes-of-aldur-guide)].

**Verisium** [CONFIRMED — [gamerant](https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-2-poe2-runes-of-aldur-league-mechanic-explained/), [fextralife](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Runes+of+Aldur), [rpgstash](https://www.rpgstash.com/blog/poe-2-runes-of-aldur-guide)]: a metal that drops from **all** Remnants regardless of which reward you picked. Spent at the **Verisium Anvil** (towns/hideout) to:
  - Convert armour pieces (helmet/gloves/boots/chest) into **Runeforged** variants — they trade some flat defensive stats for **Runic Ward**, a supplementary damage-absorption pool that depletes before Life.
  - Upgrade **any Unique weapon** in the game — Runeforging raises its effective base/level requirement rather than granting Ward, making leveling uniques viable much longer [single-source — gamerant].
  - Add Ward to gear generally, per maxroll's independent description [single-source — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/runes-of-aldur-overview)].

**Kalguuran skill gems** [CONFIRMED — gamerant, rpgstash]: new skill gems with **no attribute requirements** that consume **Runic Ward** instead of mana to activate.

**Alloys** [CONFIRMED — gamerant, fextralife (13 types)]: currency items that remove a random affix on gear and replace it with a **guaranteed** one — functionally an Essence-adjacent, targeted-affix crafting tool, applied per equipment slot family.

**Legacy Runes / Aldur's Legacy** [single-source — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/runes-of-aldur-overview)]: a system that lets you destroy a Unique item to convert it into a powerful "Legacy Rune." Not corroborated elsewhere — treat as real but under-documented.

**Fluxes** [CONFIRMED — [u4n](https://www.u4n.com/news/how-to-get-and-use-resistance-fluxes-in-poe-2.html), multiple search-corroborated summaries]: league-exclusive currency that **converts** one elemental resistance type on an item to a different type. Fluxes do **not add** a resistance that isn't already present — if the item has no Fire res roll, a Flux cannot create one; it only retypes an existing resistance roll. Obtained from Runes of Aldur farming (specific recipe/drop unconfirmed — see Open Questions) or bought on trade.

**Saga items** — Omens (stack size 10, drop level 78) that alter the next **Expedition Logbook** you open, turning ordinary Grand Expedition biomes into scripted high-value encounters [CONFIRMED via recipe cross-listing — [aoeah rune recipes](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4602--poe-2-05-rune-recipes-list-and-locations), [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-aldurs-saga-guide), [aoeah saga guide](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4670--poe-2-best-saga--saga-types-farming-crafting-when-and-how-to-use)]:

| Saga | Recipe (runes) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Aldur's Saga** | Wisdom + Vision + Life + Ward + Power + Arcane + Time | Next Logbook's Grand Expedition nodes get boosted reward mods, guaranteeing high-slot (5–8) Verisium Remnants; 8-slot remnants can roll "Runefather's/Runebinder's Alloy" (divine-tier) [single-source — timesaver] |
| **Medved's Saga** | Rage + Vision + Bloodletting + Moon + Time + Wisdom | Guarantees Medved, the Fallen Seer on next Ocean-biome Logbook reveal [single-source — game8/poe2wiki via search] |
| **Uhtred's Saga** | Ward + Vision + Stone + Arcane + Wisdom + Time | Guarantees a fight with **Uhtred, the Stardrinker** — widely called the single highest-value expedition boss target; exclusive rare drop pool incl. lineage gems and a Mana Rune quest item reported at **~700–900+ Divine** per drop [single-source, numbers vary between sources — [aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4670--poe-2-best-saga--saga-types-farming-crafting-when-and-how-to-use), search-summarized game8] |
| **Vorana's Saga** | Moon + Vision + Bond + Time + Wisdom + Toxic | Guarantees Vorana, Last to Fall |
| **Olroth's Saga** | Tempest + Vision + Time + Celestial + Wisdom + Power | Guarantees Olroth, Origin of the Fall (also a pinnacle-tier boss, see §8) |

Acquisition of the Sagas themselves is RNG-gated: dropped from specific runeword combinations resolving at a Remnant, farmed reliably via the unique map **Moor of the Fallen Skies** (reached through the "Fallen Stars" rumour in Expedition Logbooks) [single-source — [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-aldurs-saga-guide)].

## 2. Essences

**Tier system** [CONFIRMED — [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-essence-guide), [u4gm](https://www.u4gm.com/path-of-exile-2/essences-currency-guide), [poe2wiki](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Essence_of_Flames)]: 0.5 uses **Lesser / Normal / Greater / Perfect / Corrupted** tiers (note: one source states the base rework to 4 tiers — Lesser/Normal/Greater/Perfect — happened in patch 0.3.0; Corrupted is a 5th tier layered on top via Vaal-orbing an imprisoned Greater-essence monster [single-source, tier-count discrepancy flagged — timesaver vs u4gm]).
- Items can hold **only one** Essence-granted modifier at a time in 0.5 [single-source — mobalytics search summary].
- 19 base Essence types, each guarantees a specific modifier family.
- **Imprisoned Essence monsters**: frozen enemies you free by clicking 3 times; they become empowered by the Essence, and killing them drops that Essence. Appear from Act 1 campaign through endgame Atlas [CONFIRMED — timesaver, poe2wiki].
- **Corrupting a Greater-Essence imprisoned monster with a Vaal Orb** (~1% success chance per source) upgrades it to a **Corrupted Essence** monster — the chase tier [single-source — timesaver].
- The 4 corrupted essences and rough trade value (0.5-era, single source, treat as a snapshot not a floor): **Hysteria** (~400 Exalted, highest value), **Horror** (~230 Exalted, "increased effect of Socketed Items" on gloves/boots), **Delirium** (~90–100 Exalted), **Insanity** (~15–20 Exalted, cheapest) [single-source — [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-essence-guide)].
- Lesser/Normal essences are near-worthless individually (~1 Exalted or less) and typically bulk-sold ~20:1 [single-source — timesaver].
- Essences are also purchasable from the **Currency Exchange NPC** per-Act, unlocked in Cruel difficulty after finishing Act 3 Normal [single-source — search-summarized game8/poe2wiki].
- Specific essence-type → specific monster/zone mapping was **not found** in sources searched — essences appear to be generic imprisoned-monster drops rather than zone-locked, contradicting a monster-specific loot-table assumption. Flag as open question.

## 3. Strongboxes

[CONFIRMED spawn-rate change — [switchbladegaming](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/path-of-exile-2/strongbox-guide/) + independently corroborated framing in search-aggregated results]: In 0.5.0, GGG **lowered base spawn rates** for Strongboxes (and Essences, Shrines, other map content) because the redesigned Atlas Passive Tree now provides much larger bonus availability — i.e., strongbox density is now Atlas-tree-gated, not baseline.

**Tier ranking by currency efficiency** [single-source — switchbladegaming]:
- **S**: Researcher's — always prioritize for currency; roll "of Bounty" (IIQ).
- **A**: Cartographer's (Atlas/Waystone sustain), Ornate (SSF/base crafting, "of Treasures" IIR).
- **B**: Jeweller's (accessory hunting), Large (generic fallback).
- **C**: Blacksmith's, Armourer's, Arcane (base-type-specific only).
- **D**: Standard — skip entirely.

**Numbers** [single-source, small sample — switchbladegaming]: community 60-map test with a minimal Atlas setup averaged **~0.5 Exalted Orbs per map** prioritizing Researcher's boxes.

**Farming recipe** [single-source — switchbladegaming]: take the "Likely Ambush" Atlas node (mandatory given lowered 0.5.0 base rates) + "Researcher's Plunder" (+150% conversion to Researcher's variant); roll boxes to Rare with Alchemy Orbs; add suffixes with Exalted only if prefixes allow; prioritize IIQ over IIR on currency boxes. Sweet spot for character IIR is **100–130%** (diminishing returns above); Atlas IIR keeps scaling without the same penalty curve.

## 4. Shrines

[CONFIRMED — [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/shrines), poe2wiki]: Shrines (aka Precursor Artifacts) buff nearby monsters; killing them and interacting grants the buff to the player, typically lasting **45s** by default.
- **Places of Worship** (Atlas passive): +100% chance for maps to contain Shrines.
- **Prayer for Guidance**: +100% chance Shrines are **Seek Shrines** (item rarity).
- **Faith in Luck**: 25% chance for a Shrine to roll an additional effect (raises odds of getting **Speed Shrines**).
- Meta strategy: stack Places of Worship to pair Speed/Seek Shrines with Strongbox farming for faster clears + higher rarity output [single-source — original combined search summary, switchbladegaming-adjacent].

## 5. Azmerian Wisps (core mechanic, active during Runes of Aldur)

[CONFIRMED — [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/azmerian-wisps), [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-azmerian-wisp-farming-guide), [vulkk 0.5.2 notes](https://vulkk.com/2026/06/12/path-of-exile-2-update-0-5-2-changes-overview/)]: Not a new 0.5 mechanic — Wisps originate from an earlier patch's league ("Dawn of the Hunt") and are now a **permanent core Atlas mechanic** that keeps running during Runes of Aldur.
- Wisps flee from the player, speeding up when approached and slowing when you back off; despawn if you lose them too long.
- A Wisp empowers monsters it passes through with animal-type buffs, then possesses a Rare/Unique monster, granting it a new ability.
- Killing a Wisp-possessed monster is the **only source of Talismans** — consumables socketed into gear Rune sockets.
- **0.5.2 "On the Wind" Atlas Keystone** [single-source — vulkk]: +50% Wisp movement speed (good for fast clear builds) or removes the despawn timer entirely (good for slow builds) — pick one.

## 6. Trials (Sekhemas + Chaos) — trade-relevant loot

### Trial of the Sekhemas
[single-source, one deep fetch — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/trial-of-the-sekhemas), corroborated at title/topic level by conquestcapped/skycoach in search snippets]
- **Access**: Djinn Barya (drops from monsters starting Act 2). Floors scale with area level: lvl 24–44 → 1 floor, 45–59 → 2, 60–74 → 3, 75+ → 4 floors.
- **Honour**: a secondary "health" bar; hitting 0 ends the run. Armour reduces Honour loss, Evasion prevents it outright; Shrines inside the Trial restore it; "Honour Resistance" stat mitigates loss.
- **Sacred Water**: run-scoped currency spent at Merchants on Boons and Relics; unspent Sacred Water converts to extra Keys at the Keth Forge.
- **Relics**: meta-progression items slotted into a Relic Altar pre-run; craftable to Magic rarity; 3 relic sizes with distinct affix pools.
- **Zarokh, The Temporal** (final boss): always drops one Unique Relic; killing him under the right Unique Relic condition unlocks a themed reward.
- **Unique Relic → reward pairs**: The Burden of Leadership → Sekhema's Resolve; The Changing Seasons → Sandstorm Visage; The Desperate Alliance → Against the Darkness (jewel); The Peacemaker's Draught → extra Djinn Barya; The Remembered Tales → Blessed Bonds; **The Last Flame → Temporalis** (sets max Honour to 1 as the drawback — high-skill, high-reward run).
- **Trade-relevant drops**: Time-Lost Jewels (exclusive to this content), Grand Spectrum Jewels, standard jewels from reward chests. No "soul cores" drop from Sekhemas — those are Trial of Chaos exclusive (see below); the domain brief's assumption that Sekhemas drops soul cores is **not supported** by sources.

### Trial of Chaos
[single-source deep fetch — [maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/trial-of-chaos)]
- **Access**: Inscribed Ultimatum item, drops from monsters starting Act 3. Rounds scale with area level: 39–59 → 4 rounds, 60–74 → 7 rounds, 75+ → 10 rounds.
- **Format**: pick one persistent negative modifier before each round; fixed sequence of kill/survive/escort/boss encounters; **failing any challenge loses all loot gathered that run** (all-or-nothing, unlike most PoE endgame content).
- **Soul Cores** are the headline reward, dropped at rounds 4, 7, and 10 (boss rounds). 15 varieties exist. **Soul Core of Azcapa** and **Soul Core of Zalatl** are called out as so valuable that community advice is to abandon (bank) the run immediately on getting one rather than risk losing it to a later failure [single-source — maxroll].
- **Bahlak, The Sky Seer** appears at round 10 and drops one of the 3 "Fates" needed to unlock the pinnacle fight.
- **Trialmaster access** requires all 3 Fates: Cowardly Fate (Uxmal, The Beastlord), Deadly Fate (Bahlak, The Sky Seer), Victorious Fate (Chetza, The Feathered Plague).
- **Trialmaster unique drops**: Glimpse of Chaos, Zerphi's Genesis, Mahuxotl's Machination, Hateforge, The Adorned, or corrupted Inscribed Ultimatums (i.e., the access item itself can drop corrupted, letting you re-roll a harder/better run).

## 7. Corruption Altars / Vaal side areas

[CONFIRMED — [epiccarry](https://epiccarry.com/blogs/path-of-exile-2-vaal-corruption-altar-guide/), [maxroll corruption outcomes](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/corruption-outcomes), [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-vaal-orb-guide)]:
- **Vaal Ruins (Act 2)**: earliest Altar of Corruption — free-to-experiment corruption on low-value items during the campaign without spending Vaal Orbs (some corruption there is apparently ambient/free — worth confirming exact mechanic).
- **Jiquani's Sanctum (Act 3)**: another Altar location.
- **Temple of Atzoatl** (via the **Incursion** endgame mechanic, which exists in PoE2 0.5): upgrading to **Tier 3** unlocks the **Locus of Corruption** room, which applies **double corruption** (two simultaneous outcomes) to an item — the strongest corruption tool in the game.
- **Vaal Orb mechanics**: cannot delete an item (unlike Orb of Chance); PoE2's Vaal Orbs skew toward stronger outcomes than PoE1's — lower chance of "brick" results, higher chance of powerful ones. Standard practice: finish all other crafting (quality, sockets, mods) before corrupting, since corruption is the last, irreversible step.

## 9. Gambling vendor (Alva)

[CONFIRMED, but **not a 0.5 addition**  — [aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/3749--poe-2-gambling-vendor-items-uniques-limit-and-more--path-of-exile-2-gambling-guide)]: Alva, at the Act 3 Ziggurat Encampment, is PoE2's gambling vendor — sells randomized-rarity gear (weapon/armour/jewellery) up to the highest reachable item level (~80). Purchase count is capped per Act, +1 per unique monster kill in that Act, caps at 100 stored purchases. Only **ground-drop pool** uniques are gettable this way — boss-exclusive uniques cannot drop from gambling. Note: PoE1's **Gwennen** (level-35+ gambling vendor, rarity revealed post-purchase) is a different game's NPC and does not exist in PoE2 — don't conflate the two when reading older guides.

## 10. Uncut gems economy (level 20 gems, spirit gems)

[CONFIRMED mechanics, values single-source/unverified — search-aggregated from [mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/high-level-gems), [aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/3753--poe-2-level-20-gem-drop-rate--spot--how-to-get-lvl-2021-gems)]:
- Level 20 gems only drop from **item level 82+** loot tables (zone level 81+ effectively), and even then there's a strong bias toward level 18 — level 20 is a tail outcome. Reported odds: **~1.82%** at ilvl 82, rising to **~5.26%** at ilvl 84 [single-source, precision suspicious — treat as rough order-of-magnitude].
- Reliable farm: **T15/T16 maps** rolled **Irradiated + Corrupted ground**, since both push effective monster item level to 82+.
- **Spirit gems** function like skill gems but are much rarer and reportedly trade at **~3x** the price of the equivalent-level normal skill gem [single-source — mobalytics].
- Early-0.5 currency tip repeated across guides: new/returning players should **bank low-level uncut spirit/skill gems** rather than using or vendoring them — some command real currency due to spirit-gem scarcity.
- Could not pull live price data from poe2scout.com/economy/uncutgems (page requires JS rendering not captured by fetch) — no hard current chaos/exalt/divine numbers obtained; treat gem pricing as directionally true but numerically unverified for this doc.

## Wallet warnings

- **Fluxes only retype existing resistances — they never add a missing one.** Buying a Flux to "fix" an item's resistance without first confirming that resistance type is already rolled on it is a wasted purchase.
- **Trial of Chaos is all-or-nothing**: failing any single challenge in a 10-round run **loses everything gathered**, including a banked Soul Core of Azcapa/Zalatl if you didn't bank it early — community advice is to voluntarily end the run the moment you get a top-tier Soul Core rather than push further rounds.
- **Vaal Orb corruption is irreversible** — finish all other crafting (quality, sockets/socket colors, affixes) before corrupting; corrupted items cannot be further modified by normal currency.
- **Gambling (Alva) cannot produce boss-exclusive uniques** — don't gamble-farm expecting boss-locked chase items; only the ground-drop pool is reachable this way.
- **Strongbox base spawn rates were nerfed in 0.5.0** — running old (pre-0.5) Atlas setups without the "Likely Ambush" / strongbox-boosting nodes will undershoot expected currency income; the Atlas tree, not baseline map spawns, is now the primary lever.
- **Buying Aldur's Sagas off trade to farm Grand Expeditions often doesn't math out** per timesaver's own guide — the currency cost of buying Sagas can exceed the expected value of the boosted Logbook outcome unless run in volume (10-15+ at once) with an optimized setup.
- **Level 20 gem farming has a real floor** — you need ilvl 82+ content (irradiated/corrupted T15-16 maps), not just "high tier maps." Farming lower-tier content for level 20 gems is close to a zero-EV loop.
- **Arbiter of Divinity is gated behind Arbiter of Ash AND two T15-Waystone-gated wings** — don't attempt without first confirming you can already comfortably clear Arbiter of Ash; it's an explicit prerequisite, not an alternative route.

## Profit angles

- **Researcher's Strongbox farming**: Atlas-invest into "Likely Ambush" + "Researcher's Plunder," roll boxes to Rare with IIQ suffixes via Exalted/Alchemy — reported ~0.5 Exalted/map baseline in a 60-map sample; scale by running more maps/hour rather than chasing bigger single hits [single-source numbers — switchbladegaming].
- **Uhtred's Saga rush-farming**: craft/acquire Uhtred's Saga → guarantee Uhtred, the Stardrinker on your next Expedition Logbook Ocean biome → chase the exclusive lineage-gem drop pool and the Mana Rune quest item reported at 700-900+ Divine per drop. Called out as the single highest-value per-boss target in Expedition content, at the cost of being highly RNG-dependent on getting the drop at all [single-source, high variance — aoeah/game8].
- **Aldur's Saga volume farming**: run 10-15+ Sagas in a batch against Grand Expedition Logbooks for Verisium Remnant loot + high-slot Alloy rolls; one source estimates **60-100 Divine/hour** for an optimized volume setup, but explicitly warns the math breaks if you're buying Sagas at trade price rather than self-farming them [single-source, wide claimed range — mobalytics profile guide].
- **Corrupted Essence farming**: target imprisoned Greater Essence monsters and Vaal Orb them for a ~1% shot at a Corrupted Essence; Hysteria (~400 Exalted) and Horror (~230 Exalted) are the two worth deliberately chasing — low hit rate but each success is a large, tradeable payout [single-source — timesaver].
- **Trial of Chaos Soul Core runs**: farm Inscribed Ultimatums, push to round 10 for 3 Soul Core shots per run; Azcapa/Zalatl are valuable enough that community practice is "extract on hit" rather than push for the Trialmaster fight itself unless already banked a lesser core.
- **Locus of Corruption (Temple of Atzoatl T3, Incursion)** double-corrupt play: apply this to an already-good rare/unique to get two simultaneous corruption outcomes in one irreversible pass — used by crafters chasing specific corrupted implicit + mod combos for resale, since double-corrupted "perfect" items command large premiums in most PoE economies (mechanic confirmed; specific PoE2 0.5 resale premiums not found in sources — treat the resale-premium claim as inference, not sourced).
- **Level 20 / Spirit gem flipping**: bank uncut spirit and skill gems early rather than vendor them; spirit gems trade ~3x their skill-gem-level equivalent — cheap to store, meaningful upside if the specific gem is chase-tier for a popular build.

## Adversarial verification (post-research)

- **REFUTED** — Uhtred's Saga Mana Rune quest item is worth 700-900+ Divine per drop
  → This conflates two different items. 'Uhtred's Saga' is a cheap Logbook/Omen-type item that guarantees an Uhtred encounter in your next Logbook — it trades for roughly 1 Divine (and has crashed further per player reports, e.g. '10ex to 7d'). The actual valuable item is the 'Depleted Mana Rune' quest item dropped by Uhtred, the Stardrinker (2-3% drop chance), which is charged into an 'Infused Mana Rune' (requires 10,000+ max Mana) and turned in to Farrow for the unique weapon Runeseeker's Call — THAT item is what trades for ~800 Divine (up to ~900 with good implicit rolls). The KB entry should rename the valuable item to 'Runeseeker's Call (via Depleted/Infused Mana Rune quest chain)' and note Uhtred's Saga separately as a low-value farming aid (~1 Divine). (https://www.iggm.com/news/poe-2-patch-0-5-0-runeseekers-call-hidden-quest-guide-mana-overflow-value-enchants)
- confirmed — Aldur's Saga volume farming yields 60-100 Divine per hour
  → No correction needed — figure is corroborated by multiple independent farming guides. One guide with actual tested data reports 1,070–1,350 Divine over 13 hours (≈75–100+ Divine/hour), and another reports a 60–100 Divine/hour stable range once Aldur's Saga (an Expedition Omen) upgrades a Grand Expedition Logbook to unlock up to 8 nodes. Treat 60-100/hr as the low-to-mid end of a range that runs up to ~150/hr with additional juicing (Abyss/Delirium/Ritual stacking). (https://www.mmoexp.com/News/poe-2-expedition-farming-guide-how-to-farm-75-divines-per-hour-with-maximum-juice.html)
- confirmed — Researcher's Strongbox farming averages ~0.5 Exalted Orb per map
  → No correction needed. Community 60-map testing with a minimal Atlas setup found ~0.5 Exalted/map (≈31 Exalted over 60 maps), driven mainly by Research Strongboxes (1+ Exalted each). Note this is a baseline figure — investing in the Researcher's Plunder Atlas node substantially raises the per-map average above 0.5. (https://www.switchbladegaming.com/path-of-exile-2/strongbox-guide/)
- confirmed — Corrupted Essence of Hysteria trades for ~400 Exalted and Horror for ~230 Exalted
  → No correction needed — exact figures independently confirmed in a July 2026 pricing snapshot: Essence of Hysteria ~400 Exalted (top-ranked corrupted essence), Essence of Horror ~230 Exalted (second, prized for its guaranteed socketed-items modifier on gloves/boots). Caveat: essence prices are volatile day to day, so the KB should flag these as a point-in-time snapshot, not a fixed value. (https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-essence-guide)
- confirmed — Spirit gems trade at roughly 3x the price of the equivalent level skill gem
  → No correction needed. Independently stated in a gem-value guide: Spirit gems function identically to skill gems but are rarer, and 'typically trade for three times as much as equivalent level skill gems.' Note the multiplier is an approximation that varies by specific gem/level, not a fixed ratio. (https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/high-level-gems)
- confirmed — Level 20 gem drop odds are ~1.82% at item level 82 and ~5.26% at item level 84
  → No correction needed to the two percentages themselves — both are directly quoted in a dedicated gem-drop-rate guide. Minor caveat for the KB: the source's own '(1 in 81)' annotation next to the 1.82% figure is internally inconsistent (1/81 ≈ 1.23%, not 1.82%; 1/55 ≈ 1.82%), so drop the '1 in X' framing and cite only the percentages, or flag as needing a cleaner primary-source confirmation (no GGG official drop table exists for this). (https://www.aoeah.com/news/3753--poe-2-level-20-gem-drop-rate--spot--how-to-get-lvl-2021-gems)
- confirmed — Soul Core of Azcapa and Soul Core of Zalatl are the two most valuable Trial of Chaos soul cores
  → No correction needed. Maxroll's Trial of Chaos resource explicitly singles out these two as 'especially valuable,' advising players to abandon the rest of a Trial of Chaos run if either drops rather than risk losing it on a later failed round. (https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/trial-of-chaos)
- **REFUTED** — Arbiter of Ash has ~5,000,000 HP with a hard 55% phase transition, and Arbiter of Divinity requires ~87%+ Lightning resistance
  → Split verdict: the Arbiter of Ash figures are confirmed (5.04 million HP on normal / 22.6 million on Uber, hard phase transition at 55% HP where he picks up his weapon and Phase 2 begins) — no correction needed there. The Arbiter of Divinity '87%+ Lightning resistance' figure is NOT well-supported: it appears on one lower-tier guide site (EZG) but a second independent guide (Dualshockers) states the standard resistance cap of 75% is 'crucial' for this fight and does not mention any unusual over-cap requirement. The KB should drop the specific 87% figure and instead say 'cap Lightning (and Fire/Cold) resistance at the standard 75%; some guides recommend additional lightning mitigation/exposure-immunity layers, but no consistent over-cap % is agreed upon across sources.' (https://www.dualshockers.com/path-of-exile-2-arbiter-of-divinity-pinnacle-boss-guide/)
- **REFUTED** — Fluxes are obtained from farming the Runes of Aldur league mechanic broadly, with no confirmed specific recipe
  → A specific recipe IS confirmed. Base Thaumaturgic Flux (levels 5-18) is crafted at an Ezomyte Remnant using the 3-rune combination Earth Rune + Ward Rune + Stone Rune; higher-level and specialized Flux variants (Crackling, Chilling, Blazing, Void Flux) use documented 5-6 rune combinations. The KB should replace 'no confirmed specific recipe' with the actual Ezomyte Remnant rune recipe(s). (https://www.aoeah.com/news/4606--poe-2-05-rune-remnant-recipes--runic-remnants-farm)
- confirmed — Chayula does not exist as a pinnacle boss encounter in PoE2 0.5, only as an Ascendancy name
  → Confirmed, with a small addition: in PoE2 0.5, Chayula appears as the NPC/lore figure 'The Dreamer' (tied to the Acolyte of Chayula / Archon of Chayula ascendancy nodes), not as a standalone boss fight. The current PoE2 pinnacle boss roster is Arbiter of Ash, Arbiter of Divinity, Xesht (We That Are One), Olroth, Zarohk the Eternal, King of the Mist, and the Trialmaster — no 'Chayula, Who Dreamt' fight exists in PoE2 (that Breachlord encounter is a PoE1-only boss). KB entry should say 'Chayula = ascendancy/lore NPC only, not a boss' rather than just 'Ascendancy name.' (https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/The_Dreamer)
- confirmed — Arbiter of Divinity access requires beating Arbiter of Ash then clearing Patriarch Hall and Matriarch Hall (T15 Waystones) to forge an Origin Core
  → No correction needed to the overall chain, though guides disagree on which Hall drops which key. Confirmed sequence: beat Arbiter of Ash first (gates the content) → clear Patriarch Hall and Matriarch Hall (both require Tier 15 Waystones, limited boss attempts) → their bosses (Phya/Phyx, sources conflict on which is which) drop the Origin Spark and Origin Cradle → slot both into the Precursor Reactor at the Origin Tower to forge the Origin Core → place the Core at the summit to start the fight. (https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-arbiter-of-divinity-guide)
- confirmed — Aldur's Saga is obtained only via specific runeword combinations at a Remnant, reliably farmed through the Moor of the Fallen Skies unique map
  → No correction needed. Aldur's Saga can be crafted at a 7-slot Ezomyte/Verisium Remnant using the rune combination Wisdom + Vision + Life + Ward + Power + Arcane + Time, OR selected directly as one of the reward-slot choices inside the Moor of the Fallen Skies unique map. That map is reliably obtained via the 'Fallen Stars' rumour inside Expedition Logbooks (guaranteed spawn when revealing a Grand Expedition area with that rumour), matching the 'reliably farmed' claim. (https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Aldur's_Saga)
