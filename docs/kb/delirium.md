# Delirium — Liquid Emotions & Fog Farming
**Patch: 0.5.x "Runes of Aldur" (league launched 2026-05-29; delirium overhaul in 0.5.0, further tuned in 0.5.1–0.5.4). All facts below are 0.5-era unless noted.**
**Corrections 2026-10-01 (sources):** citations to real-money-trading shops were removed; facts that lost their only independent source were re-sourced from poe2db (Delirium, Liquid_Emotions, Simulacrum, Simulacrum_Splinter, Potent_Liquid_Melancholy), the official 0.5.2/0.5.3 patch notes, game8 and maxroll, or downgraded to [single-source] / [unverified]; the Waystone-instill table was corrected against poe2db.

## 1. Core fog mechanic

- You trigger Delirium by walking through a **Delirium Mirror** in a map; this opens an expanding fog ring. You must stay inside the fog as it spreads — stepping out ends the encounter early. Depth reached inside the fog sets a **Deliriousness%** value for that map region, which increases monster count/damage/toughness and improves rewards. [single-source] (game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/605040)
- Maps start at **10% Deliriousness**. Deliriousness **carries over and stacks across fog-connected maps** (via Grand Mirror chaining, see below), up to a cap of **200%**. [CONFIRMED] (game8.co/archives/605040: "Maps covered in Delirium fog begin at 10% Delirious", "Delirious carries over and increases across all affected maps"; [poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01: "the entire map is Delirious starting at 10%. Killing Rare monsters and the Map boss increases the Deliriousness of all maps in the fog … Deliriousness can continue to be increased up to 200% delirious.")
- 0.5.0 rebuilt Delirium end-to-end: new quest ("Strange Reflections"), new hub (**The Withered Willow**), new pinnacle boss (Tangmazu), a new Delirium Atlas passive tree, Liquid Emotions for jewel crafting, and 16 new amulet-only Notables. [CONFIRMED — official-tier aggregator] (pathofexile.gg/0-5-3-patch-notes/ context + WebSearch synthesis of maxroll.gg/poe2/news/path-of-exile-2-0-5-3-patch-preview)
- **Grand Mirror**: a Grand Mirror can spawn when you kill a map boss under the influence of Delirium; it creates a **reflected duplicate of the map boss**, and you must kill both. When the bosses are defeated, Delirium fog spreads to nearby maps. [CONFIRMED] (maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/delirium: "you kill a map boss under the influence of delirium"; [poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01: "A Grand Mirror causes a reflection of the Map Boss. When the bosses are defeated Delirium fog spreads to nearby Maps."; game8.co/archives/605040). Whether the mirror itself appears on the same map or on a neighbouring Atlas map is [unverified].
- **"Trial of Madness"**: "After triggering the Trial of Madness, fog will spread from a map of your choosing on the Atlas including a locked Simulacrum. … On reaching 100% the Simulacrum will become available." This is how fog is chained across several maps and Deliriousness ramped from 10% toward 200%. [verified-primary] ([poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01). Exact trigger/interaction details (e.g. whether a tablet is needed) are [unverified].
- At/near **200% Deliriousness**, difficulty is described as "comparable to or higher than Pinnacle boss encounters". "As Delirious increases, reward quality also improves across all fog-affected maps", including Simulacrum Splinter chances. [single-source] (game8.co/archives/605040)
- 0.5.3 patch changes (official-tier source): Simulacrum Maps no longer spawn immediately when fog is spread — they spawn only once a region hits 100% Deliriousness. Fog *scaling* itself was halved for early-endgame difficulty, offset by Delirium Tablets now scaling fog effect when multiple tablets are used (3 tablets restores pre-0.5.3 scaling). The old "double tablet effect in Grand Mirror areas" notable Atlas passive was replaced with "areas with Grand Mirrors also have a Delirium Mirror." [CONFIRMED — official] (pathofexile.gg/0-5-3-patch-notes/; fog-scaling, tablet-scaling and Simulacrum-spawn lines re-checked against the [official 0.5.3 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3968601) on 2026-10-01)
- **"Delirium cities" — could not confirm this term.** No source in this research used it. The real 0.5 terminology for the chained-fog/ramp-to-200% system is **"Trial of Madness"**. Treat "delirium cities" as either an informal community nickname not indexed by search, or a misremembering — see Open Questions.

## 2. Liquid Emotions — full catalogue

Naming convention observed across sources: base noun is **"Liquid [Emotion]"**, prefixed by a tier adjective (**Diluted / [no prefix] / Concentrated / Potent / Ancient Potent**). Some guide sites use **"Distilled Emotion"** as an apparently-interchangeable synonym for the same items (particularly in amulet-anoint context) — this could not be independently confirmed as a distinct item class vs. just alternate phrasing. [unverified naming nuance]

### 2.1 Tier ladder (13 base emotion "families")

| Tier | Emotions | Legal targets |
|---|---|---|
| Diluted | Ire, Guilt, Greed | Amulet (anoint ingredient), Waystone (instill), Rare **Basic** Jewel (crafting) |
| Standard (no tier prefix) | Paranoia, Envy, Disgust, Despair | same as above |
| Concentrated | Fear, Suffering, Isolation | same as above |
| **Potent** (new in 0.5) | Contempt, Ferocity, Melancholy | Amulet (unlocks 16 *exclusive* Notables not on the passive tree), Rare Basic Jewel (crafting — Contempt grants "+1 Suffix/Prefix Modifier allowed", Ferocity "(40–60)% increased Effect of Suffixes/Prefixes"; see 2.5) |
| **Ancient** (new in 0.5; one Ancient version of every one of the 13 above — "10 Ancient" + "3 Ancient Potent") | Ancient [any of the 13] | **Rare Time-Lost Jewels ONLY** — cannot instill amulets or Waystones |

[CONFIRMED — tier list cross-matches across independent sources] (dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions, [poe2db Liquid_Emotions](https://poe2db.tw/us/Liquid_Emotions) accessed 2026-10-01 — lists Diluted Ire/Guilt/Greed, Paranoia/Envy/Disgust/Despair, Concentrated Fear/Suffering/Isolation plus Potent and Ancient variants, WebSearch synthesis of official 0.5.0 patch summary). Each emotion exists at exactly one non-Ancient potency tier — there is no "Diluted Contempt" or "Potent Ire". [single-source] (dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions)

[verified-primary] ([poe2db Liquid_Emotions](https://poe2db.tw/us/Liquid_Emotions) accessed 2026-10-01). The exact UI flow and the maximum number of emotions per Waystone (reported as 3) are [unverified].

### 2.3 Amulet instilling ("anointing")

- Location: **Decanter of Madness** at **The Withered Willow** hub, unlocked via the "Strange Reflections" quest. [CONFIRMED] (game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/604018, WebSearch synthesis via multiple guides)
- Mechanic: place an amulet in the top slot, **exactly 3** Liquid/Distilled Emotions in the slots below. The specific 3-emotion combination — **and their order** — determines the Notable Passive granted. The same 3 emotions in a different order can produce a **different** Notable. [CONFIRMED] (game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/604018, updated 2026-06-16: the Alt/Inspect view shows "the type of Liquid Emotions required and the specific order they need to be arranged at"; mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/distilled-emotions via WebSearch)
- **How to look up a combo**: open the Passive Skill Tree (P), hover any Notable, hold **Alt** (or Inspect/R3 on controller) — the game shows the exact emotion combination and order required. [CONFIRMED] (multiple sources, WebSearch synthesis)
- Regular tree Notables can be instilled this way, functioning like a deterministic PoE1-style anoint. [CONFIRMED] (game8.co/archives/604018; poe2db emotion item text: "Can be used at The Withered Willow to Instil Amulets with a Notable Passive Skill", [poe2db Diluted_Liquid_Greed](https://poe2db.tw/us/Diluted_Liquid_Greed) accessed 2026-10-01). The Loathsome Mire sub-area drops "amulet bases with instilled notables" [verified-primary] ([poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01); that these are **Twisted/Distorted** bases with exactly **2** Notables rests on the AI-aggregated Loathsome Mire sources in §4 [single-source].
- **Potent emotions unlock 16 exclusive Notables** that do not exist anywhere else on the passive tree — this is the dedicated new content of 0.5's anoint system, not just "same tree, deterministic." [CONFIRMED] (WebSearch synthesis of official patch summary + dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions paraphrase). Only two named examples surfaced in research: **Storm's Rebuke** and **Kaom's Blessing** [single-source, weak] (WebSearch/mobalytics "Hidden Anoints 0.5" guide, page itself 403'd on fetch).
- Cannot instill **corrupted** amulets. [single-source] (game8.co/archives/604018: "Corrupted amulets cannot be instilled.") The extension to **mirrored and sanctified** items, and that re-instilling replaces the previous Notable, are [unverified].
- Example standard-tree anoint recipes actually confirmed (illustrative, not exhaustive):

- Potent emotions, like the lower tiers, "Can be used at The Withered Willow to Instil Amulets with a Notable Passive Skill" and also craft Rare **Basic** Jewels. [verified-primary] ([poe2db Potent_Liquid_Melancholy](https://poe2db.tw/us/Potent_Liquid_Melancholy) accessed 2026-10-01)

Those descriptions match the **jewel crafted modifiers** poe2db lists for the Potent emotions (see 2.5), not an amulet Notable; their only "amulet" source was a real-money-trading shop guide (citation removed). ### 2.5 Jewel crafting (0.5 addition) — regular vs. Ancient

- **Regular tier (Diluted through Potent)** applied to a **Rare Basic Jewel**: "removes a random existing modifier and augments the jewel with a new guaranteed Crafted modifier" drawn from that specific emotion's mod pool (similar in spirit to PoE1 Essences). [CONFIRMED mechanic] (dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions; [poe2db Potent_Liquid_Melancholy](https://poe2db.tw/us/Potent_Liquid_Melancholy) accessed 2026-10-01: "Removes a random modifer and Augments a Rare Basic Jewel with a new guaranteed Crafted modifier")
- **Potent-tier jewel mod pools — CONFIRMED 2026-09-28** (poe2db pages fetched successfully this time, cross-checked against our datamined RePoE catalog `src/data/poe2/repoe/catalog-*.json.gz`):
  - **Potent Liquid Contempt** → crafted **"+1 Suffix Modifier allowed"** (sits in a PREFIX slot) or **"+1 Prefix Modifier allowed"** (sits in a SUFFIX slot), on Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald/Diamond. The same mods the Ancient tier grants on Time-Lost jewels — so the +1-slot craft is NOT Time-Lost-only. ([poe2db](https://poe2db.tw/us/Potent_Liquid_Contempt) accessed 2026-09-28; RePoE `base_items["Metadata/Items/Currency/EndgameDistilledEmotion3"].properties.description` + `mods.CraftedJewelAdditionalSuffixAllowed` / `CraftedJewelAdditionalPrefixAllowed`)
  - **Potent Liquid Ferocity** → crafted **"(40–60)% increased Effect of Suffixes"** (PREFIX slot) or **"(40–60)% increased Effect of Prefixes"** (SUFFIX slot). ([poe2db](https://poe2db.tw/us/Potent_Liquid_Ferocity) accessed 2026-09-28; RePoE `EndgameDistilledEmotion2` + `mods.CraftedJewelSuffixEffect` / `CraftedJewelPrefixEffect`)
  - **Potent Liquid Melancholy** → colour-keyed conditional SUFFIX (Ruby: Debilitate on Hit while Emerald+Sapphire socketed; Sapphire: Elemental Exposure while Ruby+Emerald; Emerald: Blind while Ruby+Sapphire). ([poe2db](https://poe2db.tw/us/Potent_Liquid_Melancholy) accessed 2026-09-28; RePoE `CraftedJewel*OnHitWhile*Socketed`)
  - Slot placement ("Prefix: +1 Suffix Modifier allowed") = poe2db label + RePoE `generation_type`; that the mod then opens the OTHER side is a single-primary-source reading (RePoE stat ids) backed by creator demos.
  - **Removal side (owner-tested 2026-10-02):** the crafted mod is picked, then a random removable mod goes from the side it lands on — 6/6 in the owner's Potent Contempt slams of 2026-10-01 (Sapphire with a fractured suffix, a crit-chance suffix and 2 prefixes: 5× "+1 Prefix" removed the crit suffix, 1× "+1 Suffix" removed a prefix) and ~8/8 in creator footage (LilBotQ [Hcb_HcWDUOA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hcb_HcWDUOA) 3:08–3:22, 20:21–20:32). Outcome odds ~50/50 are an estimate [unverified]; streak reports ([forum 3958506](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3958506), 3 posters) hint some item states force an outcome. Contempt at 3 mods with no removable suffix: untested [unverified].
  - **A second crafted mod is refused**: "This item already has a crafted mod." — even when the existing crafted mod has been fractured (a Concentrated Liquid Fear mod blocked Contempt) [community — [forum 3967316](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3967316), 2026-06-17, n=1]. See docs/research/poe2-crafting-knowledge.md §2, §6.
- **Ancient tier** applied to a **Rare Time-Lost Jewel** only: same "remove random mod, add guaranteed crafted mod" base mechanic, but gated behind Delirium Atlas passive unlocks and generally far pricier due to jewel scarcity/power. [CONFIRMED] (dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions, game8.co/archives/603308, game8.co/archives/603309)
- **Ancient Potent Liquid Contempt** — the flagship craft: removes a random modifier and adds a guaranteed **"+1 Prefix Modifier allowed"** or **"+1 Suffix Modifier allowed"** to a Rare Time-Lost Jewel, raising its total mod cap. This is the item the task brief's "Contempt = +1 prefix/suffix slot" refers to, and it is a **Time-Lost Jewel** craft, not an amulet effect — the non-Ancient **Potent** Contempt grants the identical pair on Rare **Basic** Jewels (see the Potent-tier bullet above). [CONFIRMED — 3 independent sources] (dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions, game8.co/archives/603309, poe2db Ancient_Potent_Liquid_Contempt accessed 2026-09-28). The rare Time-Lost jewel's normal cap is **2 prefixes + 2 suffixes** [owner trade observation 2026-10-02 — listings at 2 + 2, none at 3 + 3; creator-demonstrated — [qATcKacI83o](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qATcKacI83o) 2:25–2:34, 8:13–9:00]; a 3rd suffix only comes from this over-cap Contempt route. An earlier 3 + 3 figure came only from a gold-seller guide and is dropped.
- **Ancient Potent Liquid Ferocity** and **Ancient Potent Liquid Melancholy**: same generic remove+guaranteed-mod craft on Time-Lost Jewels, but **without** the +1 slot bonus — hence they price far below Contempt. [single-source per item, consistent mechanic] (game8.co/archives/603308, dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions) Their mods (CONFIRMED 2026-09-28, poe2db Ancient_Potent_Liquid_Ferocity / _Melancholy accessed 2026-09-28 + RePoE `CraftedJewelRadius*Resistance` / `CraftedJewelRadiusExtraLargeSize`): Ancient Ferocity = radius SUFFIX "Notable Passive Skills in Radius also grant +(5–7)% to Fire/Cold/Lightning Resistance" (Diamond +(4–5)% Chaos per poe2db + RePoE; Game8 says (5–7)% — conflict, datamine preferred) — **not** "Effect of Suffixes", which is the Potent (Basic-jewel) Ferocity mod; Ancient Melancholy = "Upgrades Radius to Very Large" (prefix).
- Both Ancient Contempt and Ancient Ferocity require the target item to be at **maximum quality** before use. [single-source, stated on both game8 pages] (game8.co/archives/603308, game8.co/archives/603309)
- **Time-Lost Jewel base types**: Ruby (martial/armour), Emerald (evasion/ranged), Sapphire (energy shield/caster), plus a unique Diamond variant; these jewels grant no inherent stats themselves — they modify other passives inside their radius. [single-source] (dadsofexile.com/liquid-emotions)
- **Drop gating**: Ancient emotions only drop after unlocking the relevant Delirium Atlas passive node(s). One node referenced by name across both game8 Ferocity/Contempt pages, **"I know your childhood fears…"**, grants "20% chance to be Ancient" whenever a Liquid Emotion drops. [CONFIRMED] (game8.co/archives/603308, game8.co/archives/603309; [poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01: "25% increased Liquid Emotions dropped by Monsters. Liquid Emotions found have a 20% chance to be Ancient Liquid Emotions")
- A separate node, **"You can't scare me anymore!"** (requires Grand Mirror activation): "Unique Monsters have a chance to drop Potent Emotions when slain within Delirium Fog". [verified-primary] ([poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01)
- Ancient/Potent items are also purchasable from the **Currency Exchange** NPC, available in every Act after completing Act 3. [single-source, stated on both game8 pages] (game8.co/archives/603308, game8.co/archives/603309)

## 3. Simulacrum & Splinters (Delirium's pinnacle gate)

- The Delirium Atlas passive **"Is this about me... or you?"** (requires Simulacrum completion): "Simulacrum Splinters can drop from Delirium Monsters in level 75+ Map Areas. Megalomaniac Jewels found have a 10% chance to allocate 3 notable passive skills". [verified-primary] ([poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01). The earlier "Tier 11+ maps" phrasing and the "single most important node" opinion lost their only source and are dropped; whether splinters can drop *without* this node in any map is [unverified].
- **300 Splinters** combine into **1 Simulacrum** ("Combine 300 Splinters to create a Simulacrum"), a map fragment applied to "any Delirium fog-covered map on the Atlas with at least 100% Deliriousness". [CONFIRMED] ([poe2db Simulacrum_Splinter](https://poe2db.tw/us/Simulacrum_Splinter) and [poe2db Simulacrum](https://poe2db.tw/us/Simulacrum) accessed 2026-10-01; maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/delirium)
- The Simulacrum is a **7-wave encounter** (cut down from 15 waves pre-0.5). [CONFIRMED — multiple independent sources] ([poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium): "Simulacrum has been updated, and is now a 7-wave encounter"; maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/delirium; sportskeeda 0.5.3 coverage)
- Deliriousness scaling inside Simulacrum: originally intended to run 0%→100% across the 7 waves (~5%/wave), but this scaling was **bugged** and not applying correctly pre-0.5.3. As of **0.5.3**, Simulacrums now correctly **start at 100% Delirious and scale up to 200%** by wave 7. [CONFIRMED — official] ([official 0.5.3 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3968601): "now start at 100% Delirious, scaling up to 200%", accessed 2026-10-01; pathofexile.gg/0-5-3-patch-notes/, sportskeeda.com/mmo/path-exile-2-0-5-3-patch-notes, maxroll.gg/poe2/news/path-of-exile-2-0-5-3-patch-preview)
- Two unique Delirium bosses can spawn mid-run: **Omniphobia, Fear Manifest** (melee) and **Kosis, The Revelation**; both are "more likely to appear in later waves". [CONFIRMED] (maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/delirium; game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/497772 — note that page still describes the pre-0.5 15-wave format). Exact wave thresholds are [unverified]. maxroll says Omniphobia "does not possess its own loot table", so the earlier claim that both bosses drop bonus splinters/rewards is dropped.
- Death penalty (reported as one free death, the second ending the run): [unverified] — its only source was a real-money-trading shop guide.
- Completing a Simulacrum gives "a key to face the new Delirium Pinnacle Boss" [verified-primary] ([poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01). That the final-wave clear also grants **2 Delirium Atlas Passive Points** (via a "Deranging Book of Knowledge") is [unverified] — seen only in a search-result excerpt, not on an opened page.
- 0.5.2 patch **halved** Unique-enemy Toughness gain and halved increased-damage for normal/magic/rare enemies inside fog: "Halved the amount of Toughness that Unique Enemies in the Fog gain during Delirium Encounters." / "Halved the amount of Increased Damage that Normal, Magic and Rare Enemies in the Fog gain during Delirium Encounters." [verified-primary] ([0.5.2 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3960375) accessed 2026-10-01)
- Simulacrum Splinters are tradeable on the Currency Exchange (poe2db shows 24h exchange rates and volume for them). [single-source] ([poe2db Simulacrum_Splinter](https://poe2db.tw/us/Simulacrum_Splinter) accessed 2026-10-01)

## 4. Loathsome Mire & Fractured Mirror Shards

- **Fractured Mirror Shards** are a special variant of the fog-progress "Mirror Shard" markers found deep in the fog; the fog-progress meter shows how deep you must go to reach one. They can add extra Liquid Emotions to nearby monsters, or summon a **"Mirrored boss"** encounter. [single-source, AI-aggregated] (WebSearch synthesis of poe2wiki.net/wiki/Loathsome_Mire + game8.co/archives/603918)
- **Loathsome Mire**: an optional side-area that can spawn from a **red** Fractured Mirror Shard. It's a large swamp escape room — you must reach and interact with an **Unethical Offering** on a central island before the rising muck kills you. Standing in the muck applies a **stacking DoT debuff** (physical + chaos, up to **10 stacks**). Reaching the Offering rewards a **Distorted Amulet or Twisted Amulet pre-instilled with 2 random Notables**, and opens a portal out. [single-source, AI-aggregated] (WebSearch synthesis of poe2wiki.net/wiki/Loathsome_Mire + game8.co/archives/603918)

## 5. Pinnacle: Tangmazu, the Raven Trickster

- Access chain (**"Strange Reflections"** questline): defeat 5 Delirium map bosses → defeat a twinned Grand Mirror boss pair → consume "Strange Fruit" to spread fog → complete a Simulacrum → place the resulting **Raven's Reflection** shard into the mirror at The Withered Willow. ## Wallet warnings

1. **Instilling a Waystone with Delirium may kill its own emotion income** — reportedly a Delirium-instilled map does not drop Liquid Emotions back. If true, farm emotions on plain maps and spend them on maps you intend to juice for other loot. [unverified] — only source was a real-money-trading shop guide; test on a cheap map first.
2. **Order matters on amulet instills.** The same 3 emotions in a different sequence can produce a *different* (or no) Notable. Always check the preview before committing — a botched instill burns rare emotions with zero refund. [CONFIRMED]
3. **Cannot instill corrupted amulets.** Verify item state before spending emotions. [single-source] (game8.co/archives/604018). Mirrored/sanctified amulets are reportedly also blocked [unverified].
4. **Ancient emotions require max-quality target items** and are one-shot, irreversible crafts. Confirm your Time-Lost Jewel base and quality before applying — especially Ancient Potent Contempt at ~8.5 Divine a pop. [single-source, both game8 pages state the quality requirement]
5. **Ancient emotions don't drop at all until you unlock the correct Delirium Atlas passive node.** Don't stockpile currency to buy Time-Lost Jewel crafts before confirming your Atlas tree actually generates the matching Ancient emotions — check the specific node text in your own tree, since exact node names/wording are only single-sourced here.
6. **Simulacrum deaths are costly.** A run can end on death (reportedly after a second death) — pushing wave 6–7 on a build that hasn't proven it can survive that Deliriousness band risks losing the Simulacrum and boss-key progress for nothing. [unverified] — exact death limit not confirmed by a non-shop source.
7. **Post-0.5.3, Simulacrums start at 100% Delirious, not 0%.** If you're using older (pre-0.5.3) guide numbers for wave-by-wave difficulty planning, they're stale — the whole run is now uniformly harder from wave 1. [CONFIRMED — official]
8. **Reforging (3→1 tier-up) is one-directional with no downgrade path.** Bulk-converting cheap common emotions into a specific rare one is efficient, but there's no way to reverse a mis-click — know exactly which top-tier emotion you need before feeding the bench. [single-source, low-confidence sourcing]
9. **The Raven-Touched Shard can't be un-socketed.** Selling it outright is usually correct unless you have your genuinely final helmet ready — socketing prematurely destroys ~80 Divine of value. [single-source] (dadsofexile.com/tangmazu)
