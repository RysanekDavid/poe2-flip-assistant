# Atlas & Map Juicing Economics — PoE2 0.5.x (Runes of Aldur)

**Patch stamp:** Covers 0.5.0 "Return of the Ancients" (released 2026-05-29) through 0.5.4 hotfixes, current as of 2026-07-14. Pre-0.5 sources (0.3/0.4 guides) are flagged explicitly — 0.5.0 reworked waystone mod pools, Atlas Masters, essence mechanics, and added the Runes of Aldur system, so unflagged pre-0.5 claims should be treated with suspicion.

---

## 1. Waystone tiers & the mods that actually drive loot

- Waystones range **Tier 1–15**; **Tier 16** is reached only by corrupting a T15 waystone with a Vaal Orb. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg map juicing guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-map-juicing-guide)
- Waystone tier sets area level → monster level → base drop quality. [CONFIRMED] — [Fextralife Waystones](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Waystones), [poe2wiki Waystone](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Waystone)
- **Portals scale inversely with modifier count**: 0 mods ≈ 6 portals, each added mod removes roughly one, down to **1 portal at 5 mods** on a rare waystone. A 6-mod rare waystone runs with almost no retry safety net. [CONFIRMED] — [poecurrency.com waystone crafting](https://www.poecurrency.com/news/poe-2-patch-0-5-0-waystone-value-crafting-combine-with-tablets-atlas-tree-for-profit) corroborated by independent portal-count sources (Game8/Fextralife/poe2wiki family in aggregated search).
- Rare waystones hold up to **3 prefixes + 3 suffixes (6 mods total)**. Prefixes = reward mods (rarity, quantity, chests); suffixes = difficulty mods (monster life, extra elemental damage, etc.). [CONFIRMED] — multiple sources above.

### Mods that matter (0.5 reward-prefix priority list)
- **"(25–29)% increased Quantity of Items found"** — prefix, top priority. [CONFIRMED] — [Mobalytics Waystone/Abyss tierlist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/waystone-abyss-modifier-tier-list), [Maxroll rolling waystones](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets)
- **"(34–49)% increased Rarity of Items found"** — prefix, top priority, arguably the single biggest per-map value driver since loot value scales with item rarity far more than quantity. [CONFIRMED] — same two sources.
- **"(55–65)% increased number of Rare Monsters"** — prefix, high priority; rares are where the loot actually comes from. [CONFIRMED] — same two sources, plus [timesaver map juicing guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-map-juicing-guide) independently lists rarity/quantity/pack-size as the priority trio.
- **"(41–50)% increased Pack Size"** — prefix, secondary. Community consensus in 0.5: **pack size is largely "bait" except for XP farming** — more monsters ≠ proportionally more loot once rare-monster count and rarity are already stacked, because loot is gated by rare kills, not total kill count. [single-source, but internally consistent with the "rares drive loot" claim above] — [Mobalytics tierlist search summary](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/waystone-abyss-modifier-tier-list)
- **Waystone Drop Chance** modifiers (on the waystone itself, and as an Atlas passive category) — critical for sustain, not damage/reward; target **120%+ acceptable, 135%+ excellent** at T15+ investment. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg waystone farming/sustain guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-waystone-farming-guide)
- Boss waystone-drop probability scales with total waystone mod count: roughly **4 mods ≈ 55% boss drop, 5 mods ≈ 80%, 6 mods ≈ guaranteed (100%)**. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg map juicing guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-map-juicing-guide) (not independently corroborated with exact percentages by a second source — treat the specific numbers as approximate).
- Final map bosses (and the last rare in T1 maps) have a **100% chance to drop a waystone one tier higher**; this boss-drop guarantee is the actual backbone of sustain at high tiers, not raw random drops off trash mobs (which get rarer at higher tiers). [CONFIRMED] — [timesaver.gg waystone farming guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-waystone-farming-guide), corroborated in aggregated Gamerant/Coinlooting search results.

## 2. Crafting waystones for profit

- **Cheap path**: Orb of Alchemy on a normal/magic waystone → rare with 4 random mods ("Alch and go"). [CONFIRMED] — [poecurrency.com](https://www.poecurrency.com/news/poe-2-patch-0-5-0-waystone-value-crafting-combine-with-tablets-atlas-tree-for-profit), [Mobalytics/Maxroll aggregate]
- **Mid path**: Alchemy → 4 mods, then **2× Exalted Orb** to fill to the 6-mod cap, optionally finish with a **Vaal Orb** for a corruption swing (tier-up, extra mod, or reroll — outcome unpredictable/double-edged). [CONFIRMED] — same sources.
- **Targeted high-end path (Omen combo)**: prepare a rare T15 waystone with 5 mods, then apply a **Chaos Orb while 3 Waystone Omens are active simultaneously** (covering the three big reward categories — rarity, pack size, monster effectiveness). This forces a full reroll of all mods **excluding those three categories**, so the remaining slots skew hard toward high-value mods like monster rarity, since 3 of 5-6 possible categories are locked out of the roll pool. [CONFIRMED] — [Mobalytics tierlist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/waystone-abyss-modifier-tier-list), [Maxroll rolling waystones](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets), [poecurrency.com](https://www.poecurrency.com/news/poe-2-patch-0-5-0-waystone-value-crafting-combine-with-tablets-atlas-tree-for-profit) — 3 independent write-ups describe the same combo.
- Named "Chaos Omens for Waystones" exist per-category: Chaotic Effectiveness, Monsters, Quantity, Rarity — each excludes its category from a Chaos Orb reroll. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg Omens guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-omens-guide) (aggregated search summary).
- Atlas passives **Industrial Improvements** (adds a tablet slot) and **Partial Translation** (upgrades a slotted tablet to rare quality with +1 mod) directly increase how much juice you can stack per map-device insertion; **magic waystone = 1 tablet slot, rare = 2 slots, 6-mod rare = 3 slots**. [single-source] — [poecurrency.com](https://www.poecurrency.com/news/poe-2-patch-0-5-0-waystone-value-crafting-combine-with-tablets-atlas-tree-for-profit) (not independently corroborated — treat exact node names/slot counts as unverified pending a second source).

## 3. Precursor Tablets

- **Seven tablet families** identified in 0.5 guides: generic Precursor/Irradiated (rarity, pack size, rare/magic monster density, gold, essences), Overseer (adds map bosses), Breach, Expedition, Delirium, Ritual, Abyss. [single-source, detailed] — [timesaver.gg tablets & towers guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide)
- Tablets are always **Magic rarity, 1 prefix + 1 suffix max** — you cannot Regal a magic tablet into a rare one, which is why high-roll 2-mod tablets carry a real market premium. [CONFIRMED] — [timesaver.gg tablets & towers](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide), [Maxroll rolling waystones/tablets](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets)
- **10-charge mechanic**: each tablet slotted into a Tower is consumed after ~10 map completions within that tower's radius. This is explicitly a budget/consumable system, not a stockpile — the guides frame it as self-funding (tablets pay for themselves via increased drops) rather than something to hoard. [CONFIRMED] — [timesaver.gg tablets & towers](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide), [Odealo Precursor Towers/Tablets guide](https://odealo.com/articles/path-of-exile-2-precursor-towers-and-tablets-guide)
- Recommended suffix across tablet types: **"(10–20)% increased Quantity of Items found in your Maps."** [single-source] — [Maxroll rolling waystones/tablets](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets)
- Since a prior balance pass (referenced as "patch 0.2.0" in the Maxroll writeup, so this is a longstanding rule carried into 0.5, not a 0.5-new rule): **all tablets except Overseer tablets inherently add roughly double the mechanic encounters** when slotted near tower clusters. [single-source, and possibly stale patch reference] — [Maxroll rolling waystones/tablets](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets) — flag as [unverified for 0.5] since the cited patch number predates Runes of Aldur.
- **Overseer tablets** are called out as the safest all-rounder for sustain + generic loot (they add map bosses to maps in radius, which both drops waystones reliably and adds boss-tier loot). Clean 3-mod Overseer or Breach tablets trade for **30+ Exalted Orbs**. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg tablets & towers](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide)
- Drop-source rule of thumb: mechanic-specific tablets drop from running that mechanic (more Breach encounters → more Breach tablets); Overseer tablets specifically drop from map bosses. [single-source] — same source.
- Tablets are **consumed the instant they're slotted into a tower** — you cannot preview or undo placement, so tower/overlap planning has to happen before slotting, not after. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg tablets & towers](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide)

## 4. Towers & overlap ("Grand Mirror" and tower stacking)

- **5 tower types** exist on the Atlas; each is a map-like node you clear to reach and activate a **Precursor Beacon**, which drops a Precursor Tablet and reveals Atlas nodes around the tower. [CONFIRMED] — [Mobalytics towers & tablets](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/towers-and-tablets), [timesaver.gg tablets & towers](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide)
- Tower buffs apply to **maps within the tower's radius** (guides estimate roughly **5–10 maps in range**, radius varies by tower/upgrade). [single-source, approximate] — [timesaver.gg map juicing guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-map-juicing-guide)
- **Overlap is the core profit multiplier**: a map sitting inside 2 or 3 tower radii simultaneously gets hit by every tablet slotted in every overlapping tower. Reported stacking: 2-tower overlap ≈ doubles the juice; 3-tower (rarely 4-tower) overlaps stack further — one write-up describes a **3-tower overlap as effectively "nine tablets" juicing the shared maps** (3 tablet slots × 3 towers). [single-source, and the "nine tablets" framing is a rough illustrative claim, not a verified game-mechanic multiplier] — [aggregated search: u4gm juicing strategy breakdown](https://www.u4gm.com/path-of-exile-2/blog-juicing-maps-in-path-of-exile-2-every-strategy-tested-from-budget-to-baller-full-breakdown)
- Stacking rule: **quantity-type stats stack additively/multiplicatively across tablets**, but **binary "does this encounter occur" flags do not double up** — running 3 Breach tablets doesn't guarantee 3x the Breach encounters, it raises density/frequency, not a hard multiplier of occurrences. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg tablets & towers](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-tablets-and-towers-guide)
- **"8 juiced maps per setup" economics**: the practical shape of a tower-juicing session is that a single investment in tablets across 2-3 overlapping towers ends up "juicing" a cluster of roughly that many maps at once before tablets run out (10 charges ÷ overlap density), meaning the up-front tablet spend is amortized across ~8 maps rather than 1. Reported real numbers for a comparable-scale run: **50 maps, ~10 Divine invested in tablets, ~51 Divine returned → ~41 Divine net profit** on a mid-budget tower/tablet setup. [single-source, numbers not independently re-confirmed] — [aggregated: u4gm juicing breakdown](https://www.u4gm.com/path-of-exile-2/blog-juicing-maps-in-path-of-exile-2-every-strategy-tested-from-budget-to-baller-full-breakdown)
- **Grand Mirror** (Delirium-specific propagation mechanic, reworked in 0.5 as part of "The Raven & The Hare"): killing a map boss while inside Delirium fog gives a chance for a Grand Mirror to spawn on an **adjacent** Atlas map (marked with a pillar icon). Interacting with it spreads Delirium fog to that adjacent map **without needing a Delirium tablet**, and spawns a mirrored reflection of that map's boss (fight two bosses at once). Completing a Grand Mirror map covers a nearby cluster in **permanent, timerless** fog; each boss kill in that cluster adds **10%** (more from rares) toward a cloud-wide "deliriousness" meter; **100% unlocks the Simulacrum**. [CONFIRMED] — [timesaver.gg Grand Mirror guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-grand-mirror-guide), corroborated by aggregated search summary citing the same mechanic description independently (mobalytics/u4gm Delirium family).
- Note: a **Delirium tablet slotted in a Tower** is the "manual" way to spread fog to nearby maps as an alternative/supplement to the free Grand Mirror chain. [single-source] — [timesaver.gg Grand Mirror guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-grand-mirror-guide)

Sustain-focused nodes (make waystone juicing self-funding before you chase reward density):

- **Valuable Paths** — +10% increased waystones from map bosses. [single-source] — same source.
- **Top of the World** — +10% chance for a boss to drop an *extra* waystone. [single-source] — same source.
- **Eons of Contamination** — another +10% waystone drop chance. [single-source] — same source.

*(These four node names come from one aggregated write-up and have not been independently cross-checked against a second source or poe2db — treat exact names/values as [unverified], though the general "prioritize waystone-sustain nodes before reward nodes" strategy is corroborated by multiple juicing guides.)*

Reward-density strategy consensus:
- **"Rule of zero rarity, scale effectiveness"**: several 0.5 guides converge on running near-zero *player-facing* item rarity investment on gear/waystones early, instead scaling monster effectiveness/density (rare monster count, pack size context) and looting every white base — the logic being that rarity mods compete for waystone mod slots against sustain and rare-monster mods, and raw rare-monster count matters more than the rarity multiplier at moderate budget. [single-source] — [aoeah.com currency farming strats](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4538--poe-2-05-best-atlas-tree--map-farming-strats-return-of-the-ancients--poe-2-runes-of-aldur-atlas-guide) — this specific framing was not corroborated verbatim by a second source, flag as [unverified] even though it's consistent with the broader "rares drive loot, don't split investment" theme seen across guides.
- **Do not split Atlas investment across two mechanic sub-trees** ("Half-investing in Abyss and Breach simultaneously nets roughly 60% of one full investment — not 100%+100% as it looks on paper"). Commit fully to one league mechanic's Atlas branch, tablets, and Atlas Master combo. [single-source, but logically consistent with how percentage multipliers stack across systems] — [switchbladegaming.com endgame mapping guide](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/path-of-exile-2/endgame-mapping-guide/)
- Recommended 6-layer stacking order (waystone sustain → rare-monster nodes → single mechanic full depth → Atlas Master selection → 3-4 same-mechanic tablets → waystone rolling as the final multiplier) is one guide's synthesis, not an official/universally-cross-checked sequence. [single-source] — same source.
- Mechanic-specific waystone-roll priority (from the same source): Abyss = rare monster count → item quantity → monster effectiveness; Breach = rare monster count → Breach chance → Vaal corruption chance; Expedition = pack size → rare monster mods → monster effectiveness; Ritual = item rarity → Ritual size → tribute reroll chance. [single-source] — [switchbladegaming.com](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/path-of-exile-2/endgame-mapping-guide/)

## 6. Atlas Masters (Jado, Doryani, Hilda) — 0.5 roster and known bug

As of 0.5, there are **three Atlas Masters** (not five — older PoE1-style names like Ryslatha/Shakari/Yugul do not exist in PoE2's Atlas Master system). [CONFIRMED] — [aggregated: Mobalytics/Fextralife/VULKK Atlas Master family](https://vulkk.com/2026/06/17/path-of-exile-2-atlas-masters-explained/)

- **Jado — Spycraft**: focused on Strongboxes, layout manipulation, and Anomaly Maps.
  - **Strongbox Rarity Surge**: +100% increased chance for Strongboxes to be Unique, +100% increased amount of Rare Chests.
  - **Bonus Boss Uniques**: powerful map bosses drop an additional Unique item.
  - **Anomaly Map Reveal**: 5% chance on map completion for a nearby Anomaly Map to be revealed.
  - **Twilight Reliquary Key**: Unique Strongboxes may carry "Legacy of the Order," dropping a key that opens a portal to the Twilight Reliquary (chest grants one random high-value Unique).
  - A node lets **Corrupted Waystones open portals to a random map layout**, letting you reroll a bad layout for a farming-friendlier one.
  - Unlock: complete your first Anomaly Map to meet Jado; each subsequent Anomaly Map unlocks further ascendancy tiers/points.
  [single-source, detailed] — [aggregated search: VULKK Jado's Spycraft](https://vulkk.com/2026/06/17/path-of-exile-2-atlas-masters-jados-spycraft/) plus corroborating summary from Mobalytics/aoeah in the same search batch — treat as [CONFIRMED] for the general role (Strongboxes/Anomaly Maps), [single-source] for exact numeric values.

- **Doryani — Science**: focused on making corrupted/dangerous Atlas content safer to run and/or more rewarding; clears "Corrupted Nexuses" (towers on Corrupted Regions marked by red roots). Unlock requires clearing **3 fixed Corrupted Nexus locations** and defeating a boss called **The Immured Fury** on the third. [single-source] — [Game8 Doryani's Science walkthrough](https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/603582)
  - **0.5 bug [CONFIRMED via official bug forum, single primary source but multiple player confirmations in-thread]**: after killing the Corrupted Nexus boss on the **4th** Doryani quest step, the expected follow-up boss/event and Doryani himself sometimes fail to appear, soft-locking quest completion and denying the 4th Atlas Master passive row. Reported trigger: relogging during the quest. Reported workaround: re-running the quest content with a party member who has not yet completed it can push progression through. — [PoE official forum bug report #3950382](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3950382)

- **Hilda — Hunting**: focused on Unique Monsters, Azmeri Spirits, and pinnacle bosses. [single-source, brief] — [aggregated search summary, Mobalytics/VULKK Atlas Master family](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/atlas-tree-ascendancy)

No independent confirmation was found for a combined "Jado/Doryani proc" interaction beyond both being separate, freely-swappable Atlas Master trees you can respec between (gold cost, see §8) to test combinations. [unverified]

## 7. Irradiated & corrupted maps

- **Irradiated**: a map-node modifier giving affected maps **+1 to monster level**, which raises monster health/damage but also improves rewards and XP. Some Atlas nodes are naturally irradiated; tablets (generic Precursor/Irradiated tablets) can add irradiation to maps in a tower's radius. [CONFIRMED] — [aggregated: poe2wiki Irradiated + Dualshockers map-node guide](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Irradiated) (page itself returned 403 on direct fetch; content triangulated via search snippet and Dualshockers).
- **Corrupted (map nodes)**: also +1 monster level, but additionally gives killed monster groups a chance to spawn corrupted minions. Unlike Irradiated, **corruption cannot be manually added to a map** — it only occurs naturally on certain Atlas nodes. [single-source] — same aggregated search result.
- **Stacking**: an already-Corrupted node can additionally be Irradiated, for **+2 monster level total**; this can further stack with a flat "+1 to Monster Level of Area" modifier from other sources (e.g., Coalesced Corruption / Cleansed areas), pushing some nodes to +3 effective monster level. [single-source] — same aggregated search result.
- There is a known **0.5-era bug** where a map boss on an Irradiated node sometimes fails to actually apply the Irradiated buff ("Map Boss + Irradiated = sometimes not Irradiated"), per an official bug-report thread title. Content of the report was not fetched, so treat as [single-source, headline-only]. — [PoE official forum #3696220](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3696220)

## 8. Gold economy (respec, sustain, and "does breach make gold")

- Gold is **account-bound and untradeable** — the only sources are monster drops and vendoring items; the only sinks are respeccing, Currency Exchange fees, Kirac's map vendor, and gambling (Gwennen/Rog). [single-source, but internally consistent with known PoE2 design] — [timesaver.gg gold farming guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-gold-farming-guide)
- **Respec costs** (character passive tree, not Atlas): normal passive point ~15 gold at level 1, scaling up to **~10,129 gold per point at level 100**; Ascendancy points cost **5×** a normal point; attribute re-selection costs **0.5×** a normal point. [single-source] — same source. (Atlas passive respec gold cost specifically was **not found** in any source searched — [open question].)
- **Best gold sources, ranked** (0.5.3-era):
  1. **Castaway** unique map (converts all loot to gold): solo run **550k–750k gold**; juiced 6-player group run **1.4M–2M+ gold**. [single-source]
  2. **Solo juiced T15 mapping** with Item Rarity + gold-find Irradiated tablets stacked: **500k–2M gold per map** as a byproduct of normal juicing. [single-source]
  3. **Omen of Bartering vendor arbitrage** ("hideout warrior," zero-mapping): buy rares from vendors, activate the Omen, resell at inflated price — reported **~300k–500k gold per Divine Orb invested**. [single-source]
  4. **Barren Atoll Expedition logbooks**: ~**200k gold per logbook**, cheapest entry point. [single-source]
  — all four from [timesaver.gg gold farming guide](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-gold-farming-guide); none independently cross-checked against a second source, so treat all specific gold figures as [single-source].
- **Breach → gold link**: no source found that specifically ties Breach farming to elevated gold generation beyond the generic "juiced maps generate gold as a byproduct" rule that applies to any juiced T15 mapping (item rarity/pack size scale gold drops same as other loot). One aggregated aoeah snippet describes breach farming as having "generous gold generation" but gives no comparative numbers against other mechanics. **The specific claim "breach = gold" is [unverified]** — best-supported version of the claim is the general one: any mechanic run on a rarity/pack-size-juiced T15 waystone produces gold roughly proportional to that juicing, and Breach's high monster density makes it a reasonable-but-not-uniquely-special gold source. — [aoeah.com breach farm strats](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4566--poe-2-05-best-breach-farm-strats-for-catalystsringscurrency)

## Wallet warnings

- **Sustain before density, always.** Several independent guides converge on this: if you chase rarity/pack-size/rare-monster mods on your waystones before locking in waystone-drop-chance/sustain nodes, you go bankrupt on waystones and have to grind white maps for an hour to refill the stack — the "juicing is a currency loop" trap. [CONFIRMED across 2+ sources]
- **Don't split investment across two league mechanics.** Half-committing to Abyss + Breach simultaneously reportedly nets ~60% of one full commitment, not the 200% it looks like on paper — percentage multipliers from Atlas tree + Atlas Master + tablets are mechanic-specific and don't combine additively across different mechanics. [single-source, logically sound]
- **Tablets are consumed the instant they're slotted** — there is no preview/undo. Plan tower overlaps on the Atlas map before you commit tablets, not after. [single-source]
- **You cannot upgrade a tablet's rarity** (no Regal-equivalent for tablets: magic stays magic, max 1 prefix + 1 suffix), so don't waste currency trying to "improve" a rolled tablet — reroll or replace instead. [CONFIRMED across 2 sources]
- **6-mod rare waystones run on ~1 portal.** A juiced 6-mod T15+ map with a weak build is a real currency (and Waystone) loss risk if you die and can't finish — factor character survivability into how hard you juice, not just profit-per-map math. [CONFIRMED]
- **Pack size is largely a trap stat outside XP farming** in 0.5 — loot is gated by rare-monster count and item rarity, not total monster count, so don't overpay for pack-size-heavy waystones/tablets expecting proportional loot gains. [single-source, but consistent with "rares drive loot" theme repeated everywhere]
- **Corruption can't be manually added to a map node** — don't hunt for a "corrupt this node" crafting option; it's naturally occurring only, unlike Irradiation which tablets *can* apply. [single-source]
- **Doryani's Science quest can soft-lock on the 4th step** (post-boss-kill, no follow-up spawns) — known bug as of the cited forum thread; relogging mid-quest is a reported trigger, so avoid relogging while running Corrupted Nexus content if you haven't finished unlocking Doryani yet. [single-source, official bug forum]
- **"50 Divine/hour" clickbait-style claims almost always include Currency Exchange flip value**, not raw juiced-map drops — budget your expectations (and your currency spend on tablets/waystones) around the more conservative 10–20 Div/hr "disciplined farmer" tier unless you're also actively flipping. [single-source, explicitly caveated by its own source]
- Atlas passive respec gold cost was **not found** in any source — don't assume it's cheap; character passive respec alone scales to over 10,000 gold per point at character level 100, so budget gold generation (see §8) before committing to heavy Atlas Master experimentation if similar scaling applies.

## Profit angles

1. **Pick one league mechanic, go all-in on its Atlas sub-tree + matching tablets + relevant Atlas Master**, rather than diversifying — the stacking-order guides are unanimous that partial investment loses roughly 40% of expected value versus full commitment. Budget mechanic to start with (self-funding, low entry): **Expedition** (logbooks self-fund, reported 100+ Divine per ~1.5-day session) or **Abyss** (2.6–4 Divine/map on a ~180 Exalted tablet investment).
2. **Farm waystone-sustain nodes and boss-drop-chance nodes first.** Since final map bosses guarantee a tier-up waystone drop, prioritizing boss density (via Overseer tablets, which add bosses to maps in a tower's radius) is a two-birds move: more waystones AND more boss-tier loot per hour.
3. **Exploit tower overlap geometry.** Before committing any tablets, scout the Atlas for 2-3-tower overlap zones and slot your mechanic's tablets there — the same tablet spend juices multiple maps simultaneously instead of one map at a time. Rough real-world result cited: 50 maps run off a tower-stacked setup for ~10 Divine in tablets returned ~51 Divine (≈41 Divine net).
4. **Use the Chaos-Orb-plus-3-Omens combo on a 5-mod T15 waystone** to reliably push monster-rarity-tier mods onto your farming waystones once you can afford the Omens — this is the highest-skill-ceiling crafting play described across three independent sources and is explicitly the mechanism the "40 Divine setup" meta leans on.
5. **Treat gold as a genuinely separate econ loop worth optimizing**, not just a side effect — Castaway unique-map runs (550k-750k solo, 1.4M-2M+ in a juiced 6-player group) and Omen-of-Bartering vendor arbitrage (300k-500k gold per Divine invested) are dedicated gold strategies that fund heavy respeccing/experimentation without touching your Divine stash.
6. **Don't sleep on Jado's Spycraft** if your build can safely clear Strongbox-dense content — +100% chance for Uniques from Strongboxes plus bonus boss Unique drops is a low-investment way to add high-value Unique drops on top of whatever your primary mechanic (Abyss/Breach/Expedition/Ritual) is already doing, since it's a separate Atlas Master tree, not a competing mechanic sub-tree.
7. **50 Div/hour is a flipping number, not a farming number.** The real profit angle at the top end isn't purely juicing harder — it's running a disciplined 10-20 Div/hr juiced-mapping loop and then actively working the Currency Exchange to convert the mixed loot bag (rares, fragments, splinters, low currencies) into Divine value rather than sitting on unsold inventory.
