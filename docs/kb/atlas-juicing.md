## 1. Waystone tiers & the mods that actually drive loot

- Waystones run **Tier 1–16**; poe2db lists "Waystone (Tier 1)" through "Waystone (Tier 16)" (area levels 65–80 for the endgame tiers). [verified-primary] — [poe2db Waystones](https://poe2db.tw/us/Waystones) accessed 2026-10-01. Corrupting a T15 with a Vaal Orb is the main way to get a **Tier 16**: Maxroll (0.2.0-era) calls it "the main method to obtain a Tier 16 Waystone" and says the corruption can move the tier up or down by one; Mobalytics suggests Vaaling a waystone "to corrupt it up to a Tier 16 Waystone". [CONFIRMED] — [Maxroll rolling waystones](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets), [Mobalytics Waystone/Abyss tierlist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/waystone-abyss-modifier-tier-list), accessed 2026-10-01. That it is the *only* route is [unverified].
- Waystone tier sets area level → monster level → base drop quality. [CONFIRMED] — [Fextralife Waystones](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Waystones), [poe2wiki Waystone](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Waystone)
- **Portals scale inversely with modifier count**: 0 mods ≈ 6 portals, each added mod removes roughly one, down to **1 portal at 5 mods** on a rare waystone. A 6-mod rare waystone runs with almost no retry safety net. [unverified] — no non-shop source states this ladder (the Fextralife page the earlier research cited does not contain it, see the adversarial log). **poe2db frames this as Revivals, not portals, and gives no per-mod count:** "The number of modifiers on a Waystone will reduce the number of Revivals available allowed when opening a Map." [verified-primary] — [poe2db Waystones](https://poe2db.tw/us/Waystones) accessed 2026-09-29. The exact 6/5/…/1 ladder stays [unverified].
- Rare waystones hold up to **3 prefixes + 3 suffixes (6 mods total)**. [CONFIRMED] — multiple sources above.
- **Corrected 2026-09-29: in 0.5 every waystone modifier bundles a difficulty effect with its rewards** — there is no "prefix = reward, suffix = difficulty" split. poe2db's 0.5 mod table lists prefix rows such as "Monsters deal (5—9)% of Damage as Extra Cold / 14% more Rarity of Items found in this Area / 15% more Waystones found in Area" and suffix rows such as "Rare Monsters have 1 additional Modifiers / 25% increased number of Rare Monsters / 10% increased Waystones found in Area"; the page intro says "Waystones can be modified to increase the difficulty and reward of monsters encountered in Maps." [verified-primary] — [poe2db Waystones](https://poe2db.tw/us/Waystones) accessed 2026-09-29. That the waystone tooltip then shows summed totals (Item Rarity, Pack Size, Monster Rarity, Monster Effectiveness, Waystone Drop Chance, Revives) is [unverified] — not stated on that page.

### Mods that matter (0.5 reward priority list)

*The reward lines below are the guides' way of naming a mod by its reward. In 0.5 each one arrives bundled with a difficulty line (see above), so none of them is a standalone "reward prefix" — [cf] guides vs [poe2db Waystones](https://poe2db.tw/us/Waystones); poe2db wins on the mod shape. The ranges below were not re-checked against poe2db's rows and stay guide-reported.*
- **"(25–29)% increased Quantity of Items found"** — prefix, top priority. [CONFIRMED] — [Mobalytics Waystone/Abyss tierlist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/waystone-abyss-modifier-tier-list), [Maxroll rolling waystones](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets)
- **"(34–49)% increased Rarity of Items found"** — prefix, top priority, arguably the single biggest per-map value driver since loot value scales with item rarity far more than quantity. [CONFIRMED] — same two sources.
- **"(55–65)% increased number of Rare Monsters"** — prefix, high priority; rares are where the loot actually comes from. [CONFIRMED] — same two sources.
- **"(41–50)% increased Pack Size"** — prefix, secondary. Community consensus in 0.5: **pack size is largely "bait" except for XP farming** — more monsters ≠ proportionally more loot once rare-monster count and rarity are already stacked, because loot is gated by rare kills, not total kill count. [single-source, but internally consistent with the "rares drive loot" claim above] — [Mobalytics tierlist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/waystone-abyss-modifier-tier-list): "So far in POE2 Pack Size is bait (with the exception of XP farming). Loot comes from Rares, and the Rare Monster Modifier along with the Rarity of Items modifiers are most important." (accessed 2026-10-01)
- **Waystone Drop Chance** modifiers (on the waystone itself, and as an Atlas passive category) — critical for sustain, not damage/reward; the "target 120%+ acceptable, 135%+ excellent at T15+" rule of thumb is [unverified] (no non-shop source; Maxroll's 0.2.0 guide gives different waystone-drop-chance targets, tied to Citadel fragment farming).
- Boss waystone-drop probability by total waystone mod count (**4 mods ≈ 55%, 5 mods ≈ 80%, 6 mods ≈ 100%**) is [unverified] — no non-shop source publishes a table like this (see the adversarial log). poe2db only says modifiers raise the tier-up chance (next bullet).
- Map bosses are the backbone of waystone sustain at high tiers. poe2db: "Only the Final Powerful Map Boss in a Map Area has a chance to drop a Waystone of a higher tier than the tier of that Map Area. The chance diminishes the higher the tier of the Waystone used to the create the Map Area, but can be increased again by adding modifiers to Waystones", and "Powerful Map Bosses frequently drop Waystones one Tier higher." [verified-primary] — [poe2db Waystones](https://poe2db.tw/us/Waystones) accessed 2026-09-29. The older guide line that final map bosses (and the last rare in T1 maps) have a **100% chance** to drop a higher-tier waystone is contradicted by that text — plan sustain around a tier-dependent chance, not a guarantee.

## 2. Crafting waystones for profit

- **Cheap path**: Orb of Alchemy on a normal waystone → rare ("Alch and go"). Maxroll: "Every Normal Waystone should be rolled with an Orb of Alchemy" for higher-tier farming. [single-source] — [Maxroll rolling waystones](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets) (0.2.0-era), accessed 2026-10-01. That the Alchemy leaves exactly 4 mods is [unverified].
- **Mid path**: after the Alchemy, **Exalted Orbs** fill the waystone to the 6-mod cap (Mobalytics budgets "+3 Exalt to max affixes"), optionally finished with a **Vaal Orb** for a corruption swing — Maxroll lists a tier move up or down by one, extra modifiers on rare waystones, or a reforge, so the outcome is double-edged. [CONFIRMED] — [Maxroll rolling waystones](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/rolling-waystones-and-precursor-tablets), [Mobalytics tierlist](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/waystone-abyss-modifier-tier-list), accessed 2026-10-01.
- **Targeted high-end path (Omen combo)**: prepare a rare T15 waystone with 5 mods, then apply a **Chaos Orb while 3 Waystone Omens are active simultaneously** (covering the three big reward categories — rarity, pack size, monster effectiveness). This forces a full reroll of all mods **excluding those three categories**, so the remaining slots skew hard toward high-value mods like monster rarity, since 3 of 5-6 possible categories are locked out of the roll pool. The exclusion mechanism follows from poe2db's omen text (next bullet). The "prepare 5 mods first" step and the "skews hard toward monster rarity" framing are [unverified]: the Maxroll page (0.2.0-era) does not describe this combo, and the Mobalytics guide describes a single-omen Chaos reroll on a waystone already filled to max affixes (re-checked 2026-10-01). Stacking three is official: the 0.5.0 notes inverted Rarity/Quantity/Monsters to exclusion, added Effectiveness, and state "Up to 3 of the above Omens may be used simultaneously." [verified-primary — [0.5.0 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540) accessed 2026-09-29]
- **Four Chaotic waystone omens, all exclusion omens** (re-verified 2026-09-29). poe2db's in-game text, verbatim, with the reward each one keeps out:
  - Omen of Chaotic Rarity: "While this item is active in your inventory your next Chaos Orb will replace all Modifiers on a Waystone with Modifiers that do not grant Item Rarity"
  - Omen of Chaotic Quantity: "… with Modifiers that do not grant Pack Size"
  - Omen of Chaotic Monsters: "… with Modifiers that do not grant Monster Rarity"
  - Omen of Chaotic Effectiveness: "… with Modifiers that do not grant Monster Effectiveness"

  [verified-primary] — [poe2db Omen_of_Chaotic_Rarity](https://poe2db.tw/us/Omen_of_Chaotic_Rarity), [Omen_of_Chaotic_Quantity](https://poe2db.tw/us/Omen_of_Chaotic_Quantity), [Omen_of_Chaotic_Monsters](https://poe2db.tw/us/Omen_of_Chaotic_Monsters), [Omen_of_Chaotic_Effectiveness](https://poe2db.tw/us/Omen_of_Chaotic_Effectiveness), all accessed 2026-09-29. **[cf]** [Game8 Omen of Chaotic Rarity](https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/550309) (updated 2026-09-10) prints "…with Modifiers that grant Item Rarity" (inclusion). poe2db is datamined from the client, so poe2db wins; the earlier "REFUTED" verdict in the adversarial log relied on Game8 and is overturned there.
- Atlas passives that raise tablet juice per map (corrected 2026-09-29): **Industrial Improvements** — "An additional Tablet may be used on City Maps" (level 75+ areas, requires completing the Burning Monolith), so it is a City-map-only extra slot, not a general one; **Partial Translation** — "Your Tablets may be upgraded to Rare and have +1 Maximum Modifier" (requires defeating the Precursor Fortress Pinnacle Boss), which changes tablet rarity, not slot count. [verified-primary] — [poe2db Industrial_Improvements](https://poe2db.tw/us/Industrial_Improvements), [poe2db Partial_Translation](https://poe2db.tw/us/Partial_Translation), accessed 2026-09-29. The tablet-slot ladder is a baseline Map Device rule, not a node effect (an earlier guide attributed it to these two nodes; poe2db's node texts above refute that). The guide's exact ladder (**magic waystone = 1 tablet slot, rare = 2 slots, 6-mod rare = 3 slots**) is [unverified]. **Official part of the ladder:** "You can use up to 3 Tablets with each Map, but the number of slots available is based on how many modifiers the Map has. Running a Map with 6 Modifiers will enable all 3 of the Tablets slots on the Map Device." [verified-primary — [0.3.1 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3862213) accessed 2026-09-29]. The thresholds for slots 1 and 2 stay [unverified]: player bug reports describe "1/3/6 modifiers to unlock each tablet slot" ([forum 3951118](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3951118)), and guides disagree.

## 3. Precursor Tablets

- **Eight tablet bases** on poe2db: Breach, Expedition, Delirium, Ritual, Irradiated (the generic one: rarity, pack size, monster density and similar map-wide lines), Overseer ("Empowers the Map Boss of a Map", 10 uses — see the Overseer bullet below), Abyss and Temple. poe2db: "Tablets are special items that can be used in the Map Device to add more Endgame Mechanics to Maps on your Atlas." [verified-primary] — [poe2db Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Tablet) accessed 2026-10-01. Temple arrived in 0.5.0: "new Temple Precursor Tablets that will guarantee Vaal Beacons in your map" [verified-primary — [0.5.0 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540) accessed 2026-09-29]. Expedition tablets can't be obtained in the Runes of Aldur league (0.5.5 notes).
- Tablets are always **Magic rarity, 1 prefix + 1 suffix max** — you cannot Regal a magic tablet into a rare one, which is why high-roll 2-mod tablets carry a real market premium. [unverified] — the shop guide that stated it was removed, and the Maxroll page (re-checked 2026-10-01) does not discuss tablet rarity or Regal Orbs.
  - **[cf] "always Magic" vs poe2db:** poe2db lists the Overseer Tablet base with `enable_rarity` "normal, magic, rare, unique" and shows two Unique Overseer Tablets ([poe2db Overseer_Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Overseer_Tablet), accessed 2026-09-29). The Partial Translation notable reads "Your Tablets may be upgraded to Rare and have +1 Maximum Modifier" ([poe2db](https://poe2db.tw/us/Partial_Translation)). Treat "tablets are always Magic" as outdated; the market premium for high-roll tablets is unaffected.
- **10 uses, one per map (corrected 2026-09-29).** Tablets go into the personal **Map Device** and apply only to the map being opened; each map opened with a tablet spends one of its 10 uses. The tower-era model in older third-party guides ("slotted into a Tower, consumed after ~10 map completions within that tower's radius") was retired in **0.3.1**: "You no longer need Towers to use Precursor Tablets, instead, Tablets can now be placed into the Map Device to add their bonuses directly to your Map." / "Each time you use a Tablet while running a Map, the number of remaining uses on the Tablet decreases. Once it reaches zero, the Tablet is destroyed." / "Non-Unique Tablets now have 10 uses (previously 12 to 18), or in the case of the Overseer's Tablet it now has 10 uses (previously 2 to 4)." [verified-primary — [0.3.1 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3862213) accessed 2026-09-29; current base text "Can be used in a personal Map Device to add modifiers to a Map" / "10 uses remaining" on [poe2db Overseer_Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Overseer_Tablet)]. Still a consumable budget, not a stockpile. The Atlas notable Curiously Durable Stone reads "8% chance to not consume Tablet uses when opening Maps" (level 75+ areas, requires defeating the Precursor Fortress Pinnacle Boss), which confirms uses are spent per map opened [verified-primary — [poe2db Curiously_Durable_Stone](https://poe2db.tw/us/Curiously_Durable_Stone) accessed 2026-09-29].
- **Three same-type tablets reforge into one** with "number of uses is the sum of the input, max 10" (at least Magic rarity) — a way to merge part-used tablets [single-source — [poe2wiki Reforging_Bench](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Reforging_Bench) accessed 2026-09-29].
- **No generic item-quantity tablet mod exists in 0.5.5 (corrected 2026-09-29).** The old Maxroll recommendation "(10–20)% increased Quantity of Items found in your Maps" is not in the 0.5.5b tablet pool. The generic loot line every tablet base can roll is the prefix "(8–12)% increased Rarity of Items found in Map"; any base can also roll the suffix "(30–40)% increased Quantity of Waystones found in Map", and the Overseer base adds "(13–20)% increased Quantity of Items dropped by Map Bosses" and "(35–60)% increased Rarity of Items dropped by Map Bosses". [verified-primary — RePoE 0.5.5b tablet mods ([repoe-fork](https://repoe-fork.github.io/poe2/), committed snapshot `src/data/poe2/regex/tablet.json`), [poe2db Irradiated_Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Irradiated_Tablet), [poe2db Overseer_Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Overseer_Tablet), accessed 2026-09-29]
- **Stacking in 0.5 is per map, by tablet type (corrected 2026-09-29).** The old Maxroll line (0.2.0 era) that tablets "add roughly double the mechanic encounters when slotted near tower clusters" describes the retired tower model. 0.5.0: "Tablets of the same type may now be used together to increase the amount of the league content that is spawned. The specifics of this are different for each content type. In some cases adding multiple tablets will increase the size of a single encounter, while in other cases it will increase the number of encounters spawned." and "Each empty tablet slot now contributes to the amount of random non-tablet spawned league content in the area." Also: "Tablets can no longer be used on maps that cannot have extra content (such as Unique Maps)." and "You can now use Orbs of Chance on Tablets." [verified-primary — [0.5.0 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540) accessed 2026-09-29]. 0.5.5 spells out two types: Expedition "1 Tablet: Adds a Kalguuran Expedition to a Map. 2 Tablets: Adds a Verisium Remnant to a Map, and 35% increased Kalguuran Expedition density. 3 Tablets: Adds an additional Kalguuran Expedition. 4 Tablets: Adds an additional Verisium Remnant, and an additional 35% increased Kalguuran Expedition density." (not obtainable in the Runes of Aldur league); Ritual "Using more than one Ritual Tablet now adds an extra Altar to a Map if possible" [verified-primary — [0.5.5 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4000864) accessed 2026-09-29].
- **Overseer tablets** as "the safest all-rounder for sustain + generic loot", and clean Overseer or Breach tablets trading for **30+ Exalted Orbs**, are [unverified] — the only source was a shop guide, and any price is league-dependent; check poe.ninja instead.
  - **What the base does.** Older tower-era guides said Overseer tablets "add map bosses to maps in radius"; that is outdated. The in-game base text is "Empowers the Map Boss of a Map 10 uses remaining" and "Can be used in a personal Map Device to add modifiers to a Map" — it strengthens the map's existing boss rather than adding bosses. Two unique Overseer Tablets exist: Season of the Hunt ("Map Bosses are Hunted by Azmeri Spirits") and Cruel Hegemony ("Map Bosses have 1 additional Modifiers"). [verified-primary] — [poe2db Overseer_Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Overseer_Tablet) accessed 2026-09-29. (poe2db's internal stat name still reads "tower add map bosses to X maps"; the player-facing text above is what counts.)
- Drop sources: Breach, Delirium, Expedition and Ritual tablets are "Dropped by monsters in" that mechanic, so more encounters mean more of that tablet; the generic (Irradiated) tablet, the Abyss tablet and the unique Overseer tablets are listed as "Dropped by monsters in maps" (Abyss also via the Currency Exchange). [single-source] — [Game8 List of Tablets](https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/487814) (updated 2026-09-10, accessed 2026-10-01). The older claim that Overseer tablets drop specifically from map bosses is [unverified]. Completing a Precursor Tower map also drops a tablet (0.3.1 notes, §4).
- **A use is spent when the map is opened, and only on a fresh map (corrected 2026-09-29).** "Tablets cannot be applied to areas that have already been attempted." [verified-primary — [0.3.1 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3862213)]. The old guide line "consumed the instant they're slotted into a tower" describes the retired tower model.

## 4. Towers, tablet stacking and the Grand Mirror

**Verdict (2026-09-29): there is no tower radius and no tower overlap in 0.5.5.** Since 0.3.1 tablets are used per map in the Map Device (§3); towers only reveal the Atlas and drop a tablet. The tower-radius/overlap model below came from guides written for the pre-0.3.1 system (or not updated since) and is kept only as a marked history note.

- **What towers do now.** "Towers can still be used to gain visibility on the Atlas, and they will also drop an extra Precursor Tablet as a reward for completing them." [verified-primary — [0.3.1 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3862213) accessed 2026-09-29]. 0.5.0 added "New Precursor Tower Maps … outside the Fortress", and the first tower completion raises the Fortress [per the 0.5.0 notes as quoted by the 2026-09-29 research pass]. The reveal radius is what the Atlas notable **Atop the World** grows ("33% increased Precursor Tower reveal radius", §5). The "5 tower types … Precursor Beacon" detail is guide-reported [single-source] — [Mobalytics towers & tablets](https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/guides/towers-and-tablets).
- **Stacking = several tablets in one map's device.** Up to 3 slots per map, all 3 open at 6 waystone mods (§2), one extra on City maps with Industrial Improvements (§2). Same-type tablets stack, with rules that differ by mechanic, and empty slots feed random league content (§3, 0.5.0 notes). The 0.5.5 Expedition ladder runs to 4 tablets, which fits a City-map fourth slot.
- **Obsolete tower-era claims still found in third-party guides (do not use):** "Tower buffs apply to maps within the tower's radius (~5–10 maps)"; "a map inside 2–3 tower radii gets every tablet from every tower — a 3-tower overlap is 'nine tablets'"; "8 juiced maps per setup" amortisation; placing tablets into a tower from the Atlas screen so overlapping radii stack (one guide updated 2026-08-08 still says this). GGG wins: the 0.3.1 notes say "You no longer need Towers to use Precursor Tablets", and poe2db's tablet text is "Can be used in a personal Map Device to add modifiers to a Map" ([poe2db Overseer_Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Overseer_Tablet)).
- Stacking nuance kept from the guides: stacking tablets raises content size or count, and encounter types capped at one per map don't multiply. That matches the 0.5.0 wording ("In some cases adding multiple tablets will increase the size of a single encounter, while in other cases it will increase the number of encounters spawned") and the 0.3.1 note on extra map content: "If you have a Modifier that adds an Expedition Encounter, which cannot have more than 1 in an area, a different type of extra content is selected instead." (0.5.5 now lets 3 Expedition tablets add a second Expedition.) [verified-primary for the wording; the per-mechanic details are not all published]
- **Tablet economics without towers:** each tablet lasts 10 maps, so its cost is spread over 10 maps, not one ("Non-Unique Tablets now have 10 uses", 0.3.1 notes, §3). [verified-primary] A run-return figure from a tower-framed shop write-up was removed 2026-10-01 (no non-shop source).
- **Grand Mirror** (Delirium-specific propagation mechanic). poe2db: "Completing Delirium Mirrors now has a chance to spawn Grand Mirrors on the Atlas on a nearby map. Grand mirrors create a duplicate of the map boss in the affected map. Killing both map bosses will unlock a new atlas mechanic: The Trial of Madness. After triggering the Trial of Madness, fog will spread from a map of your choosing on the Atlas including a locked Simulacrum. While inside the fog, the entire map is Delirious starting at 10%. Killing Rare monsters and the Map boss increases the Deliriousness of all maps in the fog. On reaching 100% the Simulacrum will become available." [verified-primary] — [poe2db Delirium](https://poe2db.tw/us/Delirium) accessed 2026-10-01. The earlier guide details — the trigger being a boss kill inside fog rather than a completed Delirium Mirror, a pillar icon, "no Delirium tablet needed", **permanent, timerless** fog, and **10% per boss kill** — are [unverified]; their only source was a shop guide, and poe2db's trigger wording differs (completing Delirium Mirrors).
  - **0.5.5 changes:** "The amount of maps the Delirium Fog spreads to from a Grand Mirror has been reduced to 10 (previously 8-14 in lower-Tier Maps, and 10-16 in high-Tier Maps)." and "The number of Delirium Monster Packs no longer scales higher beyond 100% Deliriousness." — so pushing Deliriousness past 100% no longer adds packs. [verified-primary] — [0.5.5 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4000864) accessed 2026-09-29.
- Note (corrected 2026-09-29): a **Delirium tablet** used in the Map Device adds Delirium to the one map it's used on; it doesn't spread fog to nearby maps. The Grand Mirror chain is what spreads fog across a cluster. The older guide line "Delirium tablet slotted in a Tower spreads fog to nearby maps" is the retired tower model (see the §4 verdict). Tablet purpose per poe2db: "Tablets are special items that can be used in the Map Device to add more Endgame Mechanics to Maps on your Atlas." [verified-primary — [poe2db Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Tablet) accessed 2026-10-01]

## 5. Atlas passives — currency-farming highlights

Sustain-focused nodes (make waystone juicing self-funding before you chase reward density):
- **Pathkeepers** — "15% increased Quantity of Waystones dropped by Map Bosses". [verified-primary]
- **Valuable Paths** — "50% increased Rarity of Waystones found" and "10% increased Quantity of Waystones dropped by Map Bosses"; applies to level 70+ areas, requires completing the Eastern or Western Gateway. [verified-primary]
- **Atop the World** (the source's "Top of the World") — "33% increased Precursor Tower reveal radius" and "10% increased Quantity of Waystones dropped by Map Bosses"; level 70+ areas, Eastern or Western Gateway. [verified-primary]
- **Archaeological Interest** — "10% increased Quantity of Waystones dropped by Map Bosses" and "15% increased Quantity of Tablets found"; level 70+, Eastern or Western Gateway. [verified-primary]
- **Eons of Contamination** is **not** a waystone node: "Irradiated Precursor Tablets may now be found" (level 70+, Eastern or Western Gateway). [verified-primary] — [poe2db Eons_of_Contamination](https://poe2db.tw/us/Eons_of_Contamination)

## 7. Irradiated & corrupted maps

- **Irradiated**: a map-node modifier giving affected maps **+1 to monster level**, which raises monster health/damage but also improves rewards and XP. Some Atlas nodes are naturally irradiated; Irradiated tablets can add irradiation to the map they are used on (corrected 2026-09-29: per map in the Map Device, not "maps in a tower's radius" — see §3/§4). [CONFIRMED] — [aggregated: poe2wiki Irradiated + Dualshockers map-node guide](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Irradiated) (page itself returned 403 on direct fetch; content triangulated via search snippet and Dualshockers).
- **Corrupted (map nodes)**: also +1 monster level, but additionally gives killed monster groups a chance to spawn corrupted minions. Unlike Irradiated, **corruption cannot be manually added to a map** — it only occurs naturally on certain Atlas nodes. [single-source] — same aggregated search result.
- **Stacking**: an already-Corrupted node can additionally be Irradiated, for **+2 monster level total**; this can further stack with a flat "+1 to Monster Level of Area" modifier from other sources (e.g., Coalesced Corruption / Cleansed areas), pushing some nodes to +3 effective monster level. [single-source] — same aggregated search result.
- There is a known **0.5-era bug** where a map boss on an Irradiated node sometimes fails to actually apply the Irradiated buff ("Map Boss + Irradiated = sometimes not Irradiated"), per an official bug-report thread title. Content of the report was not fetched, so treat as [single-source, headline-only]. — [PoE official forum #3696220](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3696220)

## Wallet warnings

- **Sustain before density, always.** Several independent guides converge on this: if you chase rarity/pack-size/rare-monster mods on your waystones before locking in waystone-drop-chance/sustain nodes, you go bankrupt on waystones and have to grind white maps for an hour to refill the stack — the "juicing is a currency loop" trap. [CONFIRMED across 2+ sources]
- **Don't split investment across two league mechanics.** Half-committing to Abyss + Breach simultaneously reportedly nets ~60% of one full commitment, not the 200% it looks like on paper — percentage multipliers from Atlas tree + Atlas Master + tablets are mechanic-specific and don't combine additively across different mechanics. [single-source, logically sound]
- **Every map you open with a tablet costs one of its 10 uses, and a tablet can't be added to a map you've already attempted.** Put tablets in the device before the first entry, and only on maps that can take extra content (not Unique Maps). There is no tower overlap to plan (corrected 2026-09-29). [verified-primary — 0.3.1 and 0.5.0 patch notes, §3]
- **Don't try to Regal a Magic tablet** — the guides report no Regal-equivalent for tablets, so reroll or replace a bad roll instead. [unverified — downgraded 2026-10-01, see §3] poe2db shows tablets can be Rare or Unique and the Partial Translation notable upgrades them to Rare (see §3), so the Atlas tree is the upgrade route, not currency.
- **More waystone mods = fewer revivals.** poe2db: "The number of modifiers on a Waystone will reduce the number of Revivals available allowed when opening a Map." [verified-primary] A juiced 6-mod T15+ map with a weak build is a real currency (and Waystone) loss risk if you die and can't finish — factor character survivability into how hard you juice, not just profit-per-map math. The "~1 portal at 6 mods" figure is [unverified] (see §1).
- **Pack size is largely a trap stat outside XP farming** in 0.5 — loot is gated by rare-monster count and item rarity, not total monster count, so don't overpay for pack-size-heavy waystones/tablets expecting proportional loot gains. [single-source, but consistent with "rares drive loot" theme repeated everywhere]
- **Corruption can't be manually added to a map node** — don't hunt for a "corrupt this node" crafting option; it's naturally occurring only, unlike Irradiation which tablets *can* apply. [single-source]
- **Doryani's Science quest can soft-lock on the 4th step** (post-boss-kill, no follow-up spawns) — known bug as of the cited forum thread; relogging mid-quest is a reported trigger, so avoid relogging while running Corrupted Nexus content if you haven't finished unlocking Doryani yet. [single-source, official bug forum]
- **Treat "50 Divine/hour" claims as including Currency Exchange flip value**, not raw juiced-map drops — budget your expectations (and your currency spend on tablets/waystones) conservatively; sober reddit self-reports sit around 6–20 Div/hr (community-reddit.md §5). [unverified — the 10–20 Div/hr "disciplined farmer" tier came only from a shop guide]
- Atlas passive respec gold cost was **not found** in any source — don't assume it's cheap; character passive respec alone scales to over 10,000 gold per point at character level 100, so budget gold generation (see §8) before committing to heavy Atlas Master experimentation if similar scaling applies.

## Profit angles

1. **Pick one league mechanic, go all-in on its Atlas sub-tree + matching tablets + relevant Atlas Master**, rather than diversifying — the stacking-order guides are unanimous that partial investment loses roughly 40% of expected value versus full commitment. Budget mechanic to start with (self-funding, low entry): **Expedition** (logbooks self-fund, reported 100+ Divine per ~1.5-day session [single-source]) or **Abyss** (a 2.6–4 Divine/map figure on ~180 Exalted of tablets circulates but is [unverified], §9).
2. **Farm waystone-sustain nodes and boss-drop-chance nodes first.** Final Powerful Map Bosses are the only source of tier-up waystones (a chance that falls with tier and rises with waystone mods, per poe2db — not a guarantee), so stronger bosses via Overseer tablets ("Empowers the Map Boss of a Map", 10 uses) are a two-birds move: more waystone sustain AND more boss-tier loot per hour. [verified-primary for the mechanics, strategy single-source] — [poe2db Waystones](https://poe2db.tw/us/Waystones), [poe2db Overseer_Tablet](https://poe2db.tw/us/Overseer_Tablet)
3. **Fill every tablet slot with the same mechanic (corrected 2026-09-29; tower overlap no longer exists).** Roll waystones to 6 mods to open all 3 slots, run City maps for the 4th slot if you have Industrial Improvements, and stack one mechanic's tablets. Same-type tablets scale that content, and full slots stop random off-mechanic content from diluting the map (0.5.0 notes, §3). Each tablet lasts 10 maps, so price it per map.
4. **Use the Chaos-Orb-plus-3-Omens combo on a rare T15 waystone** to push the reroll away from the excluded reward lines once you can afford the Omens. The omens' exclusion wording is verified verbatim on poe2db (§2), and 0.5.0 states "Up to 3 of the above Omens may be used simultaneously" (§2), so the mechanism stands. The "prepare 5 mods first" step and how strongly it skews toward monster rarity are [unverified] (§2).
5. **Treat gold as a genuinely separate econ loop worth optimizing**, not just a side effect — Castaway converts every drop to gold (poe2db, §8) and the Omen of Bartering makes a vendor misjudge an item's gold value (poe2db, §8). The yield figures for both (550k-750k solo Castaway runs, 300k-500k gold per Divine on Bartering) are [unverified] (§8).
6. **Don't sleep on Jado's Spycraft** if your build can safely clear Strongbox-dense content — +100% chance for Uniques from Strongboxes plus bonus boss Unique drops is a low-investment way to add high-value Unique drops on top of whatever your primary mechanic (Abyss/Breach/Expedition/Ritual) is already doing, since it's a separate Atlas Master tree, not a competing mechanic sub-tree.
7. **50 Div/hour is a flipping number, not a farming number.** The real profit angle at the top end isn't purely juicing harder — it's running a disciplined juiced-mapping loop (the 10-20 Div/hr figure is [unverified], §9) and then actively working the Currency Exchange to convert the mixed loot bag (rares, fragments, splinters, low currencies) into Divine value rather than sitting on unsold inventory.
