## Craft recipes

### Waystones / Tablets

**DIY max monster-effectiveness 8-mod waystone (Grand Expedition juicing)** — [S1]
- Base: Tier 15 waystone (any roll).
- Steps: Activate Omen of Chaotic Monsters + Omen of Chaotic Quantity + Omen of Chaotic Rarity together → roll waystone to 6 mods → apply Chaos Orb → yields a "pure" 70% monster-effectiveness 6-mod waystone. Optionally corrupt (Vaal Orb) for a chance at 8-mod/tier 16; if it drops to tier 14, vendor it (never run tier-14 for this strategy).
- Materials: T15 waystone, Omen of Chaotic Monsters ×1, Omen of Chaotic Quantity ×1, Omen of Chaotic Rarity ×1, Chaos Orb ×1, Vaal Orb ×1 (optional).
- Economics: Core juicing tool, not resold — value realized via map loot (see Grand Expedition strategy). Personal-best map on this exact waystone: 41 raw divine from monster drops alone.

**Easier half-effect/half-rarity waystone (cheaper alternative)** — [S1]
- Base: Tier 15 waystone.
- Steps: Omen of Chaotic Quantity + Omen of Chaotic Rarity only (omit Chaotic Monsters) → roll to 6 mods → Chaos Orb.
- Materials: T15 waystone, Omen of Chaotic Quantity ×1, Omen of Chaotic Rarity ×1, Chaos Orb ×1.
- Economics: Easier maps, still lucrative; recommended for weaker/lower-budget builds.

**5-Mod "Monster Rarity" Waystone (Abyss juice)** — [S7]
- Base: ~20-40 rare waystones kept in inventory.
- Steps: Reroll each to exactly 4 modifiers → use exactly one Exalted Orb to reach 5 mods (hard 5-mod threshold guarantees 13% monster rarity + bonus "chaotic rarity/effectiveness/quantity" stats — only 5 possible mods exist at this tier that include monster rarity). Do NOT corrupt — rarity already maxed, tablets add 16-20 more mods anyway, so corruption isn't worth the currency.
- Materials: ~20-40 rare waystones, reroll currency, 1 Exalted Orb per waystone.
- Economics: Not resold — consumed as farming input for the Abyss City-Map Grand-Mirror strategy below.

### Boots

**ilvl82 Spirit/ES/Chaos-Res boots (fracture + chaos-spam + perfect-exalt + Desecrated Mark of the Abyss)** — [S2]
- Base: Rare ilvl82 boots (~80 div), suffix pool restricted to resistances only at this ilvl (guarantees any suffix hit = wanted resistance).
- Steps: Fracture a favorable T1 flat-ES roll (settle for 59/60 rather than risk more Divine Orbs chasing 60) → Chaos-Orb-spam remaining affixes toward the wanted partial state (~31 chaos average) → Perfect Exalted Orb slam for the final prefix/suffix (re-slam if it lands wrong side) → apply the ~15 divine crafted-mod prerequisite that unlocks Desecrated crafting → Desecrate to add "Mark of the Abyss" (flat Spirit, generated at ilvl1 regardless of host ilvl — works even on low true-ilvl bases; known UI bug requires multiple clicks) → optional Vaal Orb corruption for bonus quality/sockets.
- Materials: ilvl82 boot base (~80 div), Fracturing Orb, Divine Orb (multiple), Chaos Orbs (bulk), Perfect Exalted Orb (~3 div), Omen for double-slam exalt, ~15 div crafted-mod prerequisite, Desecration/Abyss bone fodder, Vaal Orb.
- Economics: All-in ~80-100 div; comparable market listing seen at 800 div (mocked as scam price); result (7 mods, 42% quality, 3 sockets after corruption) judged "probably worth a mirror." Warning: once publicized, copies can crash to ~5 div.

**Putrefaction Boots (6× Desecrated Mod Slam)** — [S10]
- Base: Rare boots, NOT corrupted, NOT already desecrated, quality 20%, sockets added.
- Steps: Quality to 20% (Armourer's Scrap) and add sockets BEFORE desecrating (corruption locks quality/sockets afterward) → apply Preserved Rib while Omen of Putrefaction is active → converts ALL existing mods into 6 desecrated mods (3 prefix/3 suffix) and corrupts the item → reveal at Well of Souls (prefixes resolve first, then suffixes; picking a low roll of a mod type can block a better roll of that type later — save an acceptable 25-30% MS for the LAST prefix) → prefix priority: flat ES > % ES > Movement Speed (never <30%, ideally 35%); suffix priority: elemental res > chaos res > rarity > attributes → socket Iron Rune (good flat roll) or Resistance Rune (weak flat roll).
- Materials: Rare boots base (~5 exalt), Omen of Putrefaction (~8 exalt), Preserved Rib (~8 exalt), ~14 Armourer's Scrap (~11 exalt), Iron/Resistance Rune (~4 exalt), Artificer's Orb. Total ~36 exalt (~0.5-0.6 div) per attempt.
- Economics: A batch of boots costing ~5 div total broke even after selling just one "mid" result; ~35% MS hits are the average outcome.

**Putrefaction Body Armour (6× Desecrated Mod Slam)** — [S10]
- Base: Rare body armour (3-mod rares fine, all mods get overwritten), NOT corrupted/desecrated, 20% quality, 2 sockets.
- Steps: Same Omen of Putrefaction + Preserved Rib combo as boots; prefix priority flat Evasion/ES ≈ % Ev/ES > hybrid flat+%ES (T3-T4 hybrid ≈ T1 single) > hybrid %ES/Ev+Life > flat Life (T3+ only) > Spirit (only once defenses secured); suffix priority elemental res > deflection > chaos res > attributes > ES recharge (low, nerfed).
- Materials: Rare body armour base (~1 exalt), Omen of Putrefaction (~8 exalt), Preserved Rib (~8 exalt), ~14 Armourer's Scrap (~11 exalt), 2× Iron/Resistance Rune (~8 exalt). Total ~36 exalt.
- Economics: At least ~1 divine average profit per attempt; recorded batch sold at 1, 2, 6, 6, 10, 13 divine; best result (~230 ES/45% elemental res/35% MS/rarity) listed ~18 div.

Expensive variant uses city-biome maps (+24% tablet effect) with 4× high-rolled tablets (both Hiveblood- and Wombgift-quant, ~45-60% each) plus optional Delirium up to 200% (loot-only, doesn't add Hiveblood).
- Materials: 3-4× Breach tablets (cheap ~9 exalt each, or ~1-2 div/matched pair for high rolls), 6-mod T15 waystone, Lavish Wombgifts (free Breach drop).
- Economics: ~30 div/hour at ~100k Hiveblood/hour (~8-9 maps/hr); up to 60+ div/hour at ~200k/hr with a faster character; city setup reaches 100k Hiveblood in as few as ~7 maps. ~2 div/map incidental ground loot on top.

**Genesis Tree Amulet womb — Absent amulet fracture** — [S3]

- Materials: Hiveblood, Ornate Wombgifts, Fracturing Orb (only on a +3 hit).

## Mechanics facts by domain

### currency-core
- **Fracturing Orb** locks one existing modifier permanently (never removable/rerollable afterward), chosen essentially at random from the item's current mods — cited odds range from ~1-in-3 to ~1-in-5 depending on total mod count at time of fracture [S2, S4, S11, S12, S20]. It CANNOT fracture a Desecrated modifier — that affix slot is excluded from its target pool [S12]. Decide whether to keep Divine-Orb-rerolling toward the max roll BEFORE fracturing, since a fractured mod's numeric value is also locked [S2].
- **Divine Orb** only rerolls the numeric value within an already-rolled tier range, never the mod type [S2, S12, S20]; can require multiple uses to hit the very top of a range (60 vs 59 flat ES cited) [S2]. Described as the single most expensive step in a high-end jewel craft when polishing several ranged mods at once [S20].
- **Chaos Orb** rerolls only non-fractured mods, and only on an affix side that still has open room — targeting an already-full side does nothing (a "has no space for more mods" message appears) [S12, S20]. Rerolling a rare that already has one affix filled is inefficient if you want the OTHER side next — roughly half of rerolls land on the already-filled side again, averaging ~2 rerolls per correct-side hit [S2, S12]. Comes in Greater/Perfect tiers (Greater guarantees ~modifier-level-35+, Perfect guarantees top-tier ~50+) [S12].
- **Exalted Orb** adds one new random mod to an open prefix/suffix slot; side is not controllable without an omen [S2, S4]. Also tiered: Greater and Perfect Exalted Orb improve the odds of a higher-tier result [S12]; Perfect Exalted Orb draws from an effectively item-level-50 mod pool regardless of the base's true ilvl, narrowing the pool and raising hit odds for low-availability mods [S8]. A "double slam" (Omen + exalt) adds two mods in one action for no real observed downside vs a single slam [S4].
- **Omen of Sinistral/Dextral Exaltation (and Necromancy for desecration)**: Sinistral forces the next action to prefix, Dextral forces suffix (or vice versa depending on the specific omen family) — used throughout to target which affix side an Exalted Orb slam or a desecration bone lands on [S4, S9, S12, S20]. Omen of Greater Exaltation makes the next Exalted Orb apply two mods instead of one — worth using instead of buying a Greater Exalted Orb directly whenever Greater Exalted Orbs are priced above (1 Exalted + 1 omen) combined [S12].
- **Omen of Catalysing Exaltation**: paired with a Perfect/Greater Exalted Orb, biases (does not guarantee) the roll toward a Catalyst's associated mod category [S8, S16].
- **Catalysts**: add "quality" that multiplicatively amplifies only the matching modifier category (e.g. Caster Catalyst amplifies caster mods, a Tempering/Prismatic-type variant amplifies defense/resistance mods) [S8, S16, S20]. Per one creator's own (unconfirmed) testing, standard quality ~doubles category weighting, 40% (max) quality ~triples it [S12]. On amulets, Catalyst quality does not show as a visible "Quality: X%" tag — it only inflates displayed numeric stat values; sellers apply it purely for cosmetic listing appeal [S12]. High enough quality can round a modifier up a whole tier (34% quality cited to bump +3 to +4 Spell Skills) [S15]. Normal rings/amulets cap at 20% quality; Breach Rings can reach 50% [S15].
- **Orb of Annulment** removes one random modifier; a genuine 50/50-or-worse gamble with 2+ mods present [S2, S4, S12, S20]. If only ONE mod exists on the targeted affix side, the removal is guaranteed to hit that specific mod — a deliberate "safe" targeting technique when a base was bought with exactly one filler mod on that side [S20]. Omen of Sinistral/Dextral Annulment forces removal to a specific affix side (prefix or suffix) but not which specific mod on that side if multiple exist [S4, S8, S12].
- **Omen of Light**: forces the next removal (Chaos/Annulment) to strip ONLY a Desecrated/crafted modifier, leaving normal mods untouched — essential before removing a temporary "+1 modifier" crafted mod; skipping it can brick the item [S4, S20]. Priced ~1 div in one source [S20] but ~8.5+ div in another [S8] — reprice before planning around it.
- **Orb of Whittling** deterministically removes the item's current lowest-tier modifier, preserving better rolls between passes [S2, S12].
- **Orb of Scouring** fully resets an item to blank — reserved strictly as a last resort since it destroys all progress including fractured mods [S2, S12].
- **Distilled/Liquid Delirium currencies** (e.g. Liquid Contempt, Liquid Ferocity — names as auto-captioned) can add a "+1 suffix (or prefix) modifier" crafted mod that occupies a slot on the OPPOSITE affix side from the one it grants room on; landing this specific craft is roughly a 50/50 per application [S4, S20].
- **Essence of the Abyss / Essence of Hysteria** can force a genuine 50/50 reforge between two specific existing/possible mods without needing an omen (already a coinflip) [S12, S15].
- Fully guaranteed post-0.5-nerf rule: **an item can hold only ONE crafted mod total**, regardless of source (Greater/Perfect Essences both tag as "crafted") — pre-nerf essence-stacking to guarantee up to 5 mods no longer works [S9].

### desecration-abyss
- Bone-tier reagents scale with quality: Jawbone (weapons/quivers), Rib (armour: helmet/body/gloves/boots/shield), Collarbone, Cranium — higher grades (e.g. "Ancient") can guarantee a minimum result tier (cited "minimum tier two") unlike lower grades which have no floor [S2, S12, S20]. Omen of Abyssal Echoes is paired with bones on essentially every desecration attempt shown, though its exact mechanical effect (extra reveal/reroll?) is unconfirmed [S20].
- Desecrated mods generate at effective **item level 1** regardless of the host item's true ilvl — lets a low-ilvl base gain mods it couldn't normally roll (e.g. Spirit on an ilvl15 boot) [S2].
- The Well of Souls reveal always resolves **prefixes before suffixes**; picking a low-tier roll of a given mod type on one slot can "use up" that mod type and block a better roll of it on a later slot — save acceptable-but-not-ideal picks for the LAST slot of that affix group [S10, S20].
- Without an affix-bias omen active, a Desecrated roll has roughly a ~25% chance of landing on/consuming an unintended slot (risking a fractured or locked-in mod) [S20].
- **Omen of Putrefaction** + a Rib/Jawbone converts an ENTIRE item's mods into desecrated mods (all 3 prefix + 3 suffix) regardless of the base's starting mod count, and corrupts the item as a side effect — the item must be quality-rolled and socketed FIRST, since corruption locks out further quality/socket changes [S10]. Only works on RARE items (fails on Magic) and cannot be applied to an item that is already desecrated or corrupted [S10].
- **Essence of the Abyss** converts an existing mod into a "Mark of the Abyssal Lord" placeholder, which a Jawbone then converts into a genuine Desecrated slot — this conversion step has a known longstanding bug that can silently fail and require multiple repeated attempts [S8].
- Bow/Spear share an exclusive desecrated mod group "Amanamu" (3 possible rolls: Pierce, Attack Speed 12-18%, reduced Spirit Reservation — only Attack Speed is strong); Quarterstaffs have NO access to this pool and must roll blind from the normal (weaker) desecrated pool [S9].
- Genesis-pool ring/jewelry mods are reachable ONLY via Desecration bone-crafting, not normal affix crafting [S16].
- Limiting the Well of Souls to expose only a subset of an item's open slots (a partial "4-mod" craft) is a deliberate technique to guarantee an unwanted stat (e.g. mana) can never be rolled, since it lives in an excluded slot [S16].

### atlas-juicing
- **Grand Expedition**: best tablet setup is 3× "+1" tablets (2 extra random mods each) + at least one of increased-rare-monsters/monster-rarity/item-rarity/monster-effectiveness (the first two rated best); don't buy 3-mod tablets unless the map has a guaranteed "all 6"/"all 7" rune-slot floor. Best waystone: 8-mod T15 with max monster effectiveness; pack size showed NO observed effect on rare-monster count or remnant loot and should be ignored entirely. T16 waystones are optional/often overpriced since the Grand Expedition atlas node already grants +1 area level [S1].
- **Waystone/tablet mechanics**: a waystone needs ≥6 total modifiers to socket 3 tablets at all — a hard gate [S3]. Using a dedicated per-mechanic tablet (Ritual/Abyss/Breach) on a map DECREASES that same mechanic's own tablet-drop odds from that map; Irradiated Tablets add one random league-mechanic encounter WITHOUT this penalty and are called "the best way to farm tablets" [S16]. Tablet loadout scales by map type: 6 tablets on normal maps, only 4 on city/citadel maps [S16].
- **0.5.3 patch fix**: city-map tablet/tower-effect nodes were previously silently not applying at all (losing 24% tablet effect); now fixed, making ordinary city maps ~30-40% juicier on average from multiplicative stacking. Because of this, 50% Tower Effect (the breakpoint for a Precursor Tablet's "+2 additional mods" to become "+3") is now reached naturally on city maps without needing dedicated keystones ("Hilda"/"Jato") [S6]. "Grand Mirror" node was nerfed in 0.5.3 — no longer grants double tablet effect in a city Delirium mirror, only guarantees a "blurred mirror" [S6].
- **Breach/Abyss tablet mods**: Hiveblood-quantity (30-60% observed) is the ONLY tablet mod that increases actual Hiveblood gain; a separate Wombgift-quantity mod stacks independently. Rarity/effectiveness/pack-size mods do NOT affect Hiveblood/Wombgift yield, only incidental ground loot [S3].
- **Abyss node "Amanu"/Amanamu**: mandatory — without it, Omen of Light will not spawn from Abyss at all [S7]. Desert biome's "Survival of the Fittest" notable is currently broken for Abyss farming (replaces additional-rare-monster packs with a single doubled-effectiveness rare, drastically cutting monster density) — avoid Desert, use Mountain/Forest/Grass instead [S7].
- Atlas mastery path recommendations vary by target mechanic: "Jato" path (Unexpected Missions, Eastern Knowledge, Partial Translations, Keen Appraisal) works broadly and is the only strong option for Ritual; "Doryani" path is buffed post-0.5.3 specifically for Abyss/Breach juicing and no longer needs pairing with "Jato"/"Hilda" [S1, S6, S7].
- A **Precursor Terraformer** device/tablet can scout neighboring maps' content before running them, enabling direct pathing to Citadels/Breach hives/rare nodes instead of blind exploration [S16].

### expedition-ritual
- Grand Expedition remnants come in 3 rune colors: purple (valuable), blue (mostly filler), and a single unique gold/proliferating rune (Opulent) that carries forward permanently onto every subsequent remnant for the rest of the run once placed in the one gold slot — roughly doubling loot when active [S1]. Runes do NOT stack — rerolling into a duplicate of an already-proliferating rune wastes the reroll [S1]. Rune tier list: S-tier (Power, Death, Bond, Oath) > A-tier (Time, Rebirth) > B-tier (remaining purple) > C-tier (remaining blue, still worth grabbing when free) [S1].
- Rerolling a remnant's rune uses a cheap, self-sustainable vendor currency ("liquid verium," name uncertain); reroll always gives the highest available tier for that remnant but never below the map's guaranteed floor [S1].
- The **Outer/Aldo Saga logbook** (name uncertain) guarantees ≥5 rune slots on every remnant; without it, remnant slot counts have no guaranteed minimum and loot is notably weaker (still somewhat lucrative). Before committing one, shift an inventory item while viewing the map device to refresh/reveal the true rumor count and boss/unique-map vs normal composition; only commit on ≥4 normal rumors (boss rumors consume/replace expedition slots) [S1]. Averaging ~5 expeditions self-sustains the logbook supply [S1]. The unique map "Fallen Skies" guarantees an Outer/Aldo Saga drop and should be run UNJUICED (reward is fixed); "Vision of Paradise" lets you run it a 2nd time for a 2nd guaranteed logbook [S1].
- **In-map Expeditions require no Logbook investment at all** — normal maps generate their own Expedition Artifacts/remnants; a group questioned why anyone bothers buying Logbooks for base value [S18]. Rarer recipes (e.g. "Aldur's Legacy") are found specifically at high remnant depth (7-8-9 mods), not shallow [S18]. Remnants count up during detonation; reaching a tier-9 ("niner") remnant is the threshold for a shot at a Mirror of Kalandra/Mirror Shard drop, though repeated tier-9 pulls in one session yielded only minor consolation currency, not a mirror [S18].
- **Ritual** runs on a "Tribute" currency; a specific tablet lowers minimum Tribute cost of an altar (price-capped at 65-70 exalt by one creator) [S18]. Standard Ritual tablets (~1 div each) are evaluated on suffix (increased chance for magic/ritual monsters, more tribute) and prefix (increased pack size, more magic monsters) — pack size on the base MAP itself is called the single most important stat for this farm [S16].
- Certain Expedition map modifiers spawn "val(e) relics" granting more monsters/rares/drops but making those monsters immune to a specific damage type — if it matches your build's damage type, you deal zero damage [S1]. Also watch for increased-explosives and increased-placement-range modifiers, both meaningfully raising extractable value [S1].
- 0.5.3 patch note: Expedition received unspecified buffs, not yet quantified by the creator who tested it [S6].

### breach
- **0.5.x Breach/Genesis Tree rework**: Hiveblood (non-tradeable, per-character resource, ~100,000 cap per community sources, only from Breach) fuels the Genesis Tree. Wombgifts (Breach drops) each feed one specific Genesis Tree "womb": Lavish → Currency/Catalyst, Signet → Ring, Ornate → Amulet, Banded → Belt, Revelatory → Breachstone [S3]. As of 0.5.x, **Catalysts are no longer a monster drop** — only producible via the Genesis Tree Currency womb [S3]. Belt womb (+2 Minion Skills) is ranked lowest priority of the four wombs [S3].
- Separate 0.5-rework chain: Breach Splinters → Wombgift → Breachstone (crafted at Genesis Tree) → opens a Breach Domain escalating Hive → Sky Hive → Hive Fortress → Breachlord fight (It That Was Tul / It That Was Esh). The lower/mid-tier Breachlord ("Hive" boss) reliably drops a guaranteed Breachstone (~4.5-5 div) + a chance at a Lineage Support Gem (~10 div) — far below the true Breach chase drop, Uul-Netol's Embrace from the pinnacle boss Xesht (hundreds of div) [S15].
- Omen of Light's drop rate scales with the character's rarity% like a currency-tier drop [S7]. Even under heavily juiced conditions, Omen of Light was a genuine dry-streak item in one sample (6 drops vs 54 Chaos Orbs and 37 Abyssal Echoes) [S17].
- A large "Infinity Breach" boss (nicknamed "Thanos," S14) is gated behind accumulating a fragment-currency (~76 units per breach clear, name uncertain) and has a "bubble" shield phase that must be popped before the boss becomes vulnerable [S14].

### delirium
- Simulacrum splinters drop specifically from unique bosses spawning inside Delirium-fog encounters (not the fog/mobs generally) — a map rolling one is worth roughly half a divine "for free," occurring roughly every 2nd-3rd map [S18]. Per-map splinter benchmarks discussed in one session: ~300/map = excellent, ~200/map = good, ~135/map = mediocre [S19]. Note: dedicated Simulacrum farming badly underperformed for multiple streamers in one session (~35 splinters total, an amount a single ordinary Delirium tablet map "should" produce on its own) even though other players reported great results — patch/variance-dependent, verify before committing farm time [S19].
- The pre-0.5.3 "Delirium boss rush" cheese (rush to map end, teleport back for the mirror, return to pop all mirrors simultaneously) was patched out in 0.5.3; mirrors must now be triggered progressively while traversing the map — moderately more tedious, not a large loss [S6]. 0.5.3 also fixed a bug where Delirium monsters stopped spawning above 150% intensity (meaning 200% Delirium maps got zero bonus monsters before the fix) and buffed general Delirium (mirror encounters can now spawn rares; splinter rates increased) [S6].
- "Linear Delirium mirror" tech: clearing all outer (non-city) maps in an area with 3 Delirium tablets each, fully clearing the fog on each, gives ~1-in-2 to 1-in-3 chance per map of spawning a Grand Mirror in the adjoining city; if ALL outer maps are cleared with fog before running the city, the fog funnels entirely into the city map for near-guaranteed full coverage there [S7]. 200% Delirium via a Grand Mirror roughly doubles city-map loot at the cost of significantly higher difficulty [S7].
- Overseer tablets can roll extra rare-monster Wisps (e.g. "+2 wisps"); a Wisp possessing a rare, when killed, duplicates its accumulated "spirit" onto a Rogue Exile present in the map, letting multiple possessed-rare kills stack spirits onto exiles before funneling them into a boss fight for maximized Rite of Passage (Golden Charm) drop odds [S18].
- Rite of Passage charm has multiple Spirit variants (Cat, Owl, Wolf cited) — no universally "best" one, it's build-dependent; Cat is generally most sought/most expensive, Owl is underrated, Wolf is cheapest but can be near-BiS for specific builds (e.g. Cast on Crit) [S19].

### league-mechanics-misc
- Rogue Exiles no longer drop corrupted versions of their exceptional items as of 0.5.3 — flagged as a possibly new (unconfirmed) farming angle [S6].
- Quality appears to scale a boot's flat ES contribution (base roll + quality → final ES), but the exact formula wasn't given in source and shouldn't be hardcoded without separate confirmation [S2].
- On a percentage-Physical-Damage ("Fizz") weapon, the skill gem used often converts that Physical damage to another element (e.g. Ice Shot converts Physical → Cold, making it PoE2 0.5's most popular/"meta" bow skill) — bow crafts should prioritize Physical/Fizz + the relevant elemental penetration/increased-damage rolls to match [S9].
- Base item roll RANGE matters as much as which affix rolled: a base can span ~20 points wide on a flat stat — buying near the top of the range is "obviously really good," near the bottom is poor value even at a similar price [S9].
- No third-party jewel-content checker/simulator exists for PoE2 (unlike PoE1 timeless jewels) — buying a rare passive-tree jewel with fixed hidden granted mods (e.g. an "Oroth"-type jewel) is fully blind [S14].
- Atlas "Idols" (e.g. Rune Idols) are a separate item type from gear; in this patch there was no dedicated "Spirit" idol, forcing Spirit shortfalls to be solved via gear/jewels instead (or via leveling a Rune Idol, which loses Spirit each level-up rather than gaining) [S16].
- Boss encounters can bug out (stuck/unresponsive) — the only known fix is to exit to character select and relog into town/hideout; waiting or relogging inside the same instance does not resolve it (forfeits the map) [S15].

### economy-meta
- Exalted:Divine exchange rate can swing hugely within a single day early in a league (1 Divine = 80 Exalted "yesterday," then a sharp same-day jump observed) — reprice cross-currency flip math live rather than assuming yesterday's rate [S15].
- Mirror of Kalandra price is extremely volatile mid-league — observed swings of +800-850 div in a single day around day 10 of one league [S18], and roughly doubling (2,500→5,000 div) over the first 14 days of another challenge while the players' own bankroll only grew ~2,400 div over the same window (net zero progress toward affording one) [S19].
- Publicizing/mass-copying a viral profit-craft recipe compresses margins fast as many crafters list the same result simultaneously and undercut each other — check current base and comparable-listing prices before running any of these crafts, not just the numbers quoted here [S2, S9, S11].
- Self-crafting (buying a cheap base + applying own currency for personal gear) is typically much cheaper than buying the finished item on trade (e.g. a ~50 div wand self-craftable for ~10 div) [S19].
- Rarity/quantity-of-items rolls on crafted gear are explicitly low-value very early in a league since players prioritize fixing resistances first — value priorities shift over the league lifecycle [S10, S19].
- Chat/community price guesses can be significantly wrong vs actual market value (one item guessed ~20 div sold for only ~6 div) — check live listings, don't trust a verbal quote [S14].

## Wallet warnings

**Fracturing / Annulment risk**
- Fracturing Orb picks essentially at random among an item's current mods (roughly 1-in-3 to 1-in-5 depending on mod count) — decide on Divine-Orb value-chasing BEFORE fracturing, since the locked mod's numeric value is frozen forever [S2, S4, S11, S12, S20].
- Orb of Annulment with 2+ mods present is a real gamble that can strip the mod you wanted to keep — called out repeatedly as the single most currency-wasting step across multiple sources [S2, S4, S8, S12, S20]. Omen of Sinistral/Dextral Annulment only guarantees which SIDE is stripped, not which specific mod if multiple exist on that side [S4].
- Chaos-Orb-spamming a rare that already has one affix filled, hoping for the OTHER side, wastes roughly half your rerolls re-hitting the already-filled side [S2, S12].

**Desecration / Well of Souls risk**
- Applying Omen of Putrefaction + a bone corrupts the item — do this only AFTER quality-rolling and socketing, since corruption locks both permanently [S10]. It only works on RARE, non-desecrated, non-corrupted items [S10].
- Without an affix-bias omen, a Desecrated roll has ~25% odds of overwriting an unintended slot, risking a fractured/locked mod [S20].
- The Mark of the Abyssal Lord → Jawbone conversion has a known bug that can silently fail — budget spare bones and don't assume one attempt suffices [S8].
- Prefix/suffix reveals resolve in order (prefixes first) — accepting a low-tier roll of a mod type early can block a better roll of that same type later; save acceptable-but-suboptimal picks for the LAST slot of that group [S10, S20].

**Market / pricing risk**
- Once a craft becomes commonly known, resale of the finished item can crash fast (an 800-div comparable dropping to ~5-div copies was cited) [S2]. Always check live comparable listings before committing currency, not a remembered or quoted price [S2, S9, S11, S14].
- Mirror of Kalandra and other top-end currency prices move far faster than a normal farmer's daily income — don't peg a currency budget to a static "divs needed for a mirror" figure [S18, S19].
- Cross-currency exchange rates (Exalt:Divine) can jump sharply within a single day early-league — re-check before flip math [S15].

**Patch-risk / exploit-risk**
- The 5-mod jewel exploit (3-suffix/2-prefix over the normal 2-suffix cap) survived two patches unaddressed but is explicitly flagged by its own creator as likely unintended and patch-risky [S4].
- The Grand Expedition juicing buff was only ~1 week old at recording and flagged as high risk of a mid-league nerf — do not assume current returns are permanent [S1].
- Simulacrum splinter farming badly underperformed for multiple experienced players in one session despite others reporting success — verify current patch behavior before committing farm time [S19].

**Execution / mechanical risk**
- ~5% of juiced Grand Expedition maps spawn rares that hit for up to 15,000 damage with 400-500M effective HP — do not juice this hard on an underprepared character [S1].
- Certain map modifiers grant monsters immunity to a specific damage type ("val(e) relics" in Expedition) — check before engaging or you'll deal zero damage [S1].
- Boss encounters can bug out and require a full relog (exit to character select) to fix — waiting in-instance does not resolve it and forfeits the map [S15].
- The Rite of Passage Wisp-stacking sequence must be executed in the correct order (kill → carry spirits → clear trash → funnel all spirits into boss room → engage) or the entire spirit-stack bonus is lost for that attempt [S18].

**Base-selection risk**
- Buying a base near the bottom of its roll range (vs the top of an observed ~20-point span) locks in mediocre value even after a perfect craft — check roll ranges, not just base type/ilvl [S9, S20].
- For the budget desecrated-jewel craft, buying a base with its 2 wanted mods on the SAME side you'll later add the crafted "+1 modifier" to doubles your Annulment cost and risks destroying a wanted mod — always buy with wanted mods on the opposite side from where the crafted mod will land [S20].
- Filter OUT corrupted and already-desecrated items when buying bases for any Putrefaction/desecration-first craft — both conditions make the item ineligible [S10].
- Spirit (and similar Magic-only-addable mods) can only be added while an item is still Magic rarity — sequence any Spirit-hunting before upgrading to Rare, or it's foreclosed permanently [S11].

## Recipe candidates for the app

*Judged against existing keys: `jewel_suffix_push`, `bow_amanamu`, `ring_catalysing_exalt`, `amulet_fracture_plus3`, `boots_putrefaction`, `boots_putrefaction_ev`, `focus_rathpith_gamble`. Recipes that clearly duplicate an existing key's mechanic (5-mod suffix-push jewel exploit ≈ `jewel_suffix_push`; bow/spear Amanamu ≈ `bow_amanamu`; +3-skill fractured amulet ≈ `amulet_fracture_plus3`; boots Putrefaction ES/EV ≈ `boots_putrefaction`/`boots_putrefaction_ev`) are excluded below.*

1. **`armour_putrefaction`** — Putrefaction body armour (6× desecrated mod slam)
 - Base search: cheap rare EV/ES body armour base (e.g. "Sleek Jacket"-type), 3+ mods OK, NOT corrupted, NOT desecrated, 20% quality achievable, 2 open sockets.
 - Result search: rare corrupted body armour, 6 mods, high flat/% ES or Evasion + elemental res + chaos res (± Spirit/rarity jackpot).
 - Materials: Omen of Putrefaction (~8 exalt), Preserved Rib (~8 exalt), ~14 Armourer's Scrap (~11 exalt), 2× Iron/Resistance Rune (~8 exalt), Artificer's Orb.
 - Why it prints: Same ~36-exalt (~0.5-0.6 div) recipe already validated as `boots_putrefaction`, applied to the highest-demand defensive slot (body armour); sessions averaged ≥1 div profit per attempt with recorded sales at 1/2/6/6/10/13 div and a best result at ~18 div — same mechanic, second gear slot, near-zero marginal implementation cost since it reuses the boots pipeline. [S10]

2. **`gloves_projectile_plus2`** — +2 Projectile Skills gloves
 - Base search: rare glove base, high ilvl, magic-rarity variant for the alt-spam route (Method 2) or rare with open affixes for Method 1.
 - Result search: rare gloves, "+2 to Level of all Projectile Skills" (fractured) + Suppress Chance + Critical Damage Bonus + T1 flat elemental/physical damage.
 - Materials: Fracturing Orb, Chaos/Annulment Orbs, Exalted Orb (Greater/Perfect), Essence of Hysteria, Ribcage bones (Preserved/Ancient), Orb of Whittling, Regal Orb (magic-base route), rune.
 - Why it prints: Comparable finished items sold for 250 div (T3 roll) up to an estimated 500 div (full BiS/T1); big skill-defining prefix for a popular projectile archetype (Ice Shot/Twisters), and the recipe is fully specified with two alternate paths (rare-first vs magic-alt-spam-first) giving the app a cost/risk toggle. [S5]

3. **`wand_alloy_crystallisation`** — Budget cast-speed/spell-damage wand (Transcendent Alloy + Prefix Crystallisation)
 - Base search: cheap any-rarity wand with 2 open suffixes or an existing T3+ crit mod, ~5 div.
 - Result search: wand with T3+ crit chance suffix, guaranteed near-min-tier alloy prefix (e.g. cast speed), T1-ish spell-damage/elemental-gain mod.
 - Materials: Greater Essence of Seeking, an "additional crafted modifier" orb, Transcendent Alloy, Sinistral/Dextral Prefix Crystallisation omen, Essence of the Abyss + Jawbone (or raw Exalted slam) for the mana-blocker, Greater Omen of Exaltation + Exalted Orb.
 - Why it prints: Explicitly the budget/low-capital variant of a documented flip loop — ~10-30 div craft cost against a typical ~80-100 div sale, with a lucky-hit example selling 30 div craft cost for 400 div; caster wands are perennial-demand items and this is a fully mechanically specified, repeatable recipe distinct from the high-end 2-socket version (which needs ~100+ div capital and is a worse fit for an automated low-budget suggestion engine). [S8]

4. **`amulet_giga_spirit`** — Expert Spirit/global-defense/resistance amulet (Genesis-style multi-omen craft)
 - Base search: Gold or Solar amulet base, Magic rarity (must stay Magic until Spirit is secured).
 - Result search: rare amulet with Spirit (T1/T2) + global Armour/Evasion/ES + Fire Resistance + a rarity/life/ES filler roll.
 - Materials: repeated desecration bone-crafting attempts, Perfect Essence of Enhancement, Omen of Dextral Crystallisation, Omen(s) of Dextral/Greater Exaltation, Fire/Lightning Catalysts (never Cold — see mechanics notes), Omen of Catalysing Exaltation, Perfect Exalted Orb(s).
 - Why it prints: Spirit is one of the single highest-value amulet prefixes in 0.5.x (gates Arctic Armour/aura thresholds like the 40-Spirit "huge break point"); this recipe converted a ~70 div input into a ~180 div sale in the sourced session, and is meaningfully different in mechanism from the already-covered `amulet_fracture_plus3` (targets Spirit via repeated desecration rather than fracturing a "+3 skills" roll), so it fills a distinct high-value amulet archetype. [S11]

5. **`jewel_desecrated_liquid`** — Budget 5-mod desecrated jewel via Liquid Contempt/Ferocity (no Fracturing Orb)
 - Base search: cheap rare jewel (~10 exalt) with exactly 2 wanted mods on one affix side and ≤1 filler mod on the other side.
 - Result search: rare jewel, 5 total mods (2 bought + 1 delirium-fished + 1 desecrated + 1 exalted/chaos-rolled), e.g. Critical Hit Chance for Spells + Spell Damage + Recover Mana on Kill combo.
 - Materials: Liquid Contempt (~1.2 div), Liquid Ferocity (~2 div), Cranium bone + Omen of Abyssal Echoes (~2 div/attempt), Omen of Sinistral/Dextral Necromancy, Orb of Annulment (~10 div), Exalted/Chaos Orbs.
 - Why it prints: Distinct mechanism from `jewel_suffix_push` (that key is the over-cap 3-suffix/2-prefix exploit via Omens of Contempt/Ferocity + desecration double-slam; this is a simpler, cheaper, non-exploit-flagged 5-mod path with no Fracturing Orb dependency) — comparable finished jewels list from ~170 div against a total craft spend of roughly 20-30 div, and it is lower patch-risk than the exploit recipe since it isn't called out as likely-unintended. [S20]

6. **`gem_corrupt_discount`** — Cheap gem self-level + Vaal corruption discount flip
 - Base search: uncorrupted gem at level 1 (cheapest listing).
 - Result search: corrupted 21/20 (or 20/21) equivalent gem currently listed on trade.
 - Materials: level-1 gem (~3 div), 1× Vaal Orb.
 - Why it prints: Simplest possible recipe in this entire dataset — no crafting-tree complexity, one currency item, clear buy-cheap/level-for-free/gamble-once economics (~3 div in vs ~6 div market price for the finished item, ~2x on a hit). Good low-complexity addition to diversify the app beyond gear-crafting into gem-flipping, though it should be flagged as a coin-flip (real chance of no gain on the corruption). [S19]

7. **`quarterstaff_desecrate_crit`** — Quarterstaff desecrate-first crit craft
 - Base search: rare quarterstaff, priced near the TOP of its observed flat-roll range (e.g. 145-150+ of a ~130-150+ span), ~4 div.
 - Result search: staff with ~550 flat Physical ("Fizz"), 12% crit chance, Crit Damage Bonus (T2+).
 - Materials: Greater Essence of Abrasion, 1-2× (Greater) Exalted Orb, Artificer's Orb, Greater Iron Rune.
 - Why it prints: Cheapest fully-specified weapon flip in the set (~4 div base + ~1 div materials → 6-9+ div sale) with an explicit stop-loss rule ("if the desecration result is bad, stop — don't keep exalting"), making it easy to encode as a low-risk, low-capital suggestion distinct from the already-covered bow/spear Amanamu recipe (quarterstaffs structurally cannot use the Amanamu pool, so this is a genuinely separate item-class recipe, not a duplicate). [S9]

8. **`amulet_desecrated_beginner`** — Beginner desecrated amulet (jackpot Spirit/Rarity)
 - Base search: any cheap Magic-rarity amulet base (~2 div), open prefix preferred.
 - Result search: rare amulet, T1 Rarity + a defensive/attribute prefix + a desecrated jackpot roll (Spirit or extra Rarity) + 2 filler mods.
 - Materials: Perfect Augmentation, Greater Essence of Opulence, Omen of Sinistral Necromancy, a desecration bone item, Omen of Greater Exaltation + Exalted Orb.
 - Why it prints: Cheapest fully-specified amulet recipe in the dataset (~4 div total), profitable even on a non-jackpot result, with an explicit ~1-in-15 shot at a dramatically higher-value Spirit/Rarity hit — good entry-level/high-volume candidate distinct from the pricier fracture-based amulet recipe already in the app. [S11]

*Notes on exclusions*: the Genesis Tree ring/amulet/belt-womb recipes ([S3], [S16]) were deliberately left off this shortlist despite good economics, because they require a Breach-Hiveblood farming loop as a prerequisite input (not just tradeable currency), which is a materially different automation dependency than the currency-only recipes already modeled by the app's existing keys.
