# Drop sources (0.5.x)

Merged from 3 research passes covering Omens, Abyss/Ritual-chain items, and misc currency/crafting items. Confidence levels: **confirmed** (multi-source corroboration), **single-source** (one source, usually poe2wiki), **unverified** (contradicted or undocumented).

**Corrections 2026-09-29 (partial, tablet model):** how tablets are used in the Omen of Light and Essence of the Abyss rows (Abyss) and the Precursor Tablets row (Tablets) was corrected against the [0.3.1 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3862213) and docs/kb/atlas-juicing.md §3–§4: tablets go into the Map Device per map, and towers no longer hold them. The drop sources themselves were not re-checked.

**Corrections 2026-10-01 (sources):** citations to real-money-trading shops were removed from the Sources column. Hinekora's Lock was re-sourced from poe2db, Vaal Cultivation Orb gained a poe2db source, and the Precursor Tablets row was re-sourced from Game8's tablet list (which also corrected the Abyss tablet source); the Imbued Alloy row dropped to single-source, and the Hinekora "rare world drop / buy it instead" advice and the "~1-3 Vaal Cultivation Orbs per clear" figure are now unverified; conquestcapped.com (also a currency seller) was dropped from the Aldur runes row, which keeps its fextralife and game8 sources. Independent fact-check, same day: the Abyssal Bones row now says conquering the Vessel of Kulemak has a chance to drop Ancient bones (0.5.3 patch notes), not a guaranteed one; the Imbued Alloy row is [unverified] (its only source sold boosting).

**Corrections 2026-10-02 (partial, disabled omens):** the Omen of Corruption row now dates its removal to 0.5.0 (patch notes, [cf] against poe2wiki's 0.3.0); the Greater Annulment, Alchemy and Coronation rows quote the 0.3.0 notes; the Homogenising row adds the 0.5.0 Currency Exchange note (fact-checked: league drops are not stated). The rest of the file is unchanged.

## Tablets

| Item | How to farm | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Precursor Tablets (mechanic-specific, e.g. Breach/Ritual/Abyss Tablet) | Breach, Delirium, Expedition and Ritual tablets are "Dropped by monsters in" that mechanic (Breaches, Deliriums, Expeditions, Rituals). **Exception:** Abyss tablets, like the generic (Irradiated) tablet and the unique Overseer tablets, are listed as "Dropped by monsters in maps", and Abyss tablets also via the Currency Exchange [Game8 List of Tablets, updated 2026-09-10, accessed 2026-10-01: https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/487814]. ## Flagged disagreements / unresolved conflicts

- **Omen of Chaotic Effectiveness** — Game8 claims it's obtainable "by visiting the Currency Exchange NPC in every Act." This is contradicted by poe2scout's own "ritual" taxonomy and the general Ritual-sourcing convention for the whole Omen family, and no other source corroborates a vendor/NPC drop. Treated as unreliable; kept as Ritual/confirmed.
- **Distilled Emotion vs. Liquid Emotion** — sources use the two names interchangeably for what looks like the same Delirium currency family, but no source explicitly confirms they're synonyms rather than distinct pools. Flagged unverified.
- **Flawless Breachstone** — no 0.5-era acquisition chain was found in any Genesis Tree guide. Do not carry over PoE1's "Blessing of" upgrade-item assumption without further research.
- **Aldur runes (Flux/Passion/Breath/Ire/Betrayal of Aldur)** — intuitively expected to tie into the Runes of Aldur league mechanic (Runic Remnants), but the only documentation found describes them as plain world drops. Unconfirmed against patch notes; treat with caution for hunt-alert logic.
- **Omen of Light's exact mislabeled ninja category** — pass 1 describes poe.ninja filing it under "Ritual"; passes 2/3 (backed by a live poe.ninja URL check for the sibling item Head of the King) describe the actual bucket as a generic, mechanic-agnostic **"Omens"** tab. All three agree on the real source (Abyss); only the precise name of the mislabeled category differs slightly. The "Omens" description is treated as more precise since it's grounded in a confirmed live URL pattern.
- **Tecrod's Gaze** — one older poewiki.net (PoE1-style) page claims an additional Abyssal Trove/Stygian Spire source; this predates 0.5 and was not reconfirmed. Kept as a footnote on the main Abyss table row, not folded into the confirmed farm method.
