METHOD:
1) Base: item level 82 GOLD ring with a FRACTURED T1 flat elemental (cold preferred). Search: ilvl 82, not corrupted, not sanctified. Check the TIER — 40% quality can make a tier-2 look bigger. Must be tier one fractured prefix. (~60-70 div base)

The ilvl-75 desecrated prefix SHIELDS the prefixes — whittles (lowest modifier level) then only target suffixes ("whittle in peace"). An ilvl-65 pick works temporarily until whittles start targeting the prefix again, then Omen of Light until an ilvl-75 mod lands.

Third suffix = settle: all-res or rarity best; T1 single res, T1 chaos res (ilvl 81), or "damage leeched as mana" all sell to attack builds. NO BRICKS on suffix whittles — an ilvl-75 mana regen can be forced off via the suffix-chaos omen + whittle.
10) Final prefix: Omens of Light + collarbones (~10-15 of each) rerolling the desecrated prefix into T1 phys flat, T1 fire flat, or rarity (each ~1/30-1/60 per reveal, but each reveal shows 3 options twice with the Light).
